**📰 Xi Jinping Arrives in Pyongyang for State Visit to North Korea**

📝 Summary: President Xi Jinping arrived in Pyongyang this morning for a state visit to North Korea, where he was warmly welcomed by Kim Jong-un. In his signed article, Xi emphasized the deep friendship between China and North Korea, demonstrating great power diplomacy.

*💬 \"Treat neighboring countries with sincerity and maintain relationships through trust.\"*

🤔 Think about: Countries need friendship. What about between people? Do you have a friendship that bridges differences? What has it brought you?

📖 Related Book: \"Red Star Over China\" by Edgar Snow

**📰 2026 Gaokao Essay Topic \"A Love Letter to Grandma\" Sparks Discussion**

📝 Summary: This year\'s national college entrance exam featured a heartwarming essay topic: \"A Love Letter to Grandma.\" Students wrote about their grandmothers\' cooking, their gray hair, and their unconditional love. The topic values genuine emotion over writing技巧, showing a return to authenticity in education.

*💬 \"Who says a blade of grass can repay the warmth of spring sunshine?\"*

🤔 Think about: If you were to write a \"love letter\" to your family, what would you write? Think about the everyday moments you usually overlook --- which one tugs at your heart?

📖 Related Book: \"Dawn Blossoms Plucked at Dusk\" by Lu Xun

**📰 China Achieves All Three \"Pearls\" of Shipbuilding Industry**

📝 Summary: China\'s shipbuilding industry has achieved a major milestone --- mastering all three \"pearls\" of shipbuilding: aircraft carriers, large LNG carriers, and large cruise ships. From catching up to leading, Chinese shipbuilders have accomplished in decades what took developed nations over a century.

*💬 \"As heaven\'s movement is ever vigorous, so must a gentleman ceaselessly strive for self-improvement.\"*

🤔 Think about: From \"can\'t build\" to \"can build everything\" --- what journey did Chinese shipbuilding go through? Do you know anyone who spent decades perfecting one thing?

📖 Related Book: \"How the Steel Was Tempered\" by Nikolai Ostrovsky

**📰 Powerful 7.9 Magnitude Earthquake Strikes Philippines, 19 Dead 134 Injured**

📝 Summary: A powerful 7.9 magnitude earthquake struck the southern Philippines, causing building collapses and landslides. At least 19 people died and 134 were injured. China offered humanitarian aid, showing that humanity is a community with a shared future in the face of disaster.

*💬 \"Lands apart, skies shared --- we breathe the same air under the same moon.\"*

🤔 Think about: When disaster strikes, some always run towards danger. What do you think \"courage\" is? Being fearless, or being afraid but still moving forward?

📖 Related Book: \"The Ordinary World\" by Lu Yao

**📰 Zverev Wins First Grand Slam Title at French Open**

📝 Summary: Alexander Zverev won his first Grand Slam title at the French Open. After years of falling short in semifinals and finals, enduring injuries and disappointments, he never gave up. In tears after the match, he said, \"After all these years, this moment has finally come.\"

*💬 \"A fine sword is sharpened by grinding; a plum blossom\'s fragrance comes from bitter cold.\"*

🤔 Think about: Zverev waited years for his dream. Is there something you\'ve been persisting at? Would you keep going even if results don\'t show right away?

📖 Related Book: \"How the Steel Was Tempered\" by Nikolai Ostrovsky

**📰 Rare Great White Shark Footage in Mediterranean Sea Raises Conservation Questions**

📝 Summary: Scientists captured rare footage of a Great White shark in the Mediterranean Sea, a species once thought regionally extinct in these waters. The discovery brings both excitement and concern, reminding us of the urgency of marine conservation and harmony between humans and nature.

*💬 \"Drain the pond and you will catch fish, but there will be no fish next year.\"*

🤔 Think about: If we don\'t protect the ocean, the next generation may only see Great Whites in documentaries. What does environmental protection mean to you personally?

📖 Related Book: \"Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Sea\" by Jules Verne

**📰 Iran\'s World Cup Team Arrives in Mexico, Sports Transcending Politics to Deliver Hope for Peace**

📝 Summary: Despite tensions in the Middle East, Iran\'s national football team arrived in Mexico to prepare for the 2026 World Cup. Escorted by Mexican military police, the team shows how sports can bridge nations. On the pitch, there are no enemies --- only people who love football.

*💬 \"Sport has the power to change the world.\"*

🤔 Think about: When everyone talks about war and conflict, why can football still bring people together? What touches you most about the spirit of sports?

📖 Related Book: \"Red Crag\" by Luo Guangbin, Yang Yiyan
