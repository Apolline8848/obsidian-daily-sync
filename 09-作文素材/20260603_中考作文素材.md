**📰 Strive to Be Good Youth of the New Era and Run the Historical Relay Race Well**

📝 Summary: On the occasion of International Children\'s Day, President Xi Jinping encouraged young people across China to be good youth of the new era and run the historical relay race well. He urged children to set ambitions and dreams from an early age, integrating personal growth with national development.

*💬 \"If the youth are strong, the country will be strong; if the youth are wise, the country will be wise.\"*

🤔 Think about: What kind of relay baton do you think you are holding right now? What kind of person do you want to become ten years from now?

📖 Related Book: \"How the Steel Was Tempered\" by Nikolai Ostrovsky

**📰 AI Empowers Cross-Border Trade: Yiwu Businesswomen\'s \'Common Prosperity\' Story**

📝 Summary: In Yiwu, the world\'s largest small-commodity market, a group of businesswomen are using AI to do cross-border trade. AI translation helps them communicate with foreign customers in real-time, AI analytics predicts trending products, and AI livestreaming sells goods around the clock. From the era of trading chicken feathers for sugar to e-commerce, and now to the AI era, Yiwu\'s story is a microcosm of Chinese innovation.

*💬 \"If you can renovate yourself one day, then you can do so every day, and continue to do so day after day.\"*

🤔 Think about: AI helps Yiwu businesswomen trade globally without knowing English: do you think technology replaces or amplifies human abilities?

📖 Related Book: \"The Ordinary World\" by Lu Yao

**📰 Pinglu Canal Fully Filled with Water: New China\'s First River-to-Sea Canal to Open Soon**

📝 Summary: Today, the 134.2-km Pinglu Canal in Guangxi was fully filled with water as its key hubs began operation. It is the first river-to-sea canal project coordinated at the national level since the founding of New China. Capable of handling 5,000-ton ships, the canal is expected to open for navigation this September, connecting inland waterways to the Beibu Gulf.

*💬 \"Great accomplishments arise from attention to small details; a giant tree grows from a tiny sprout.\"*

🤔 Think about: A 134.2-km canal starts with the first shovel of earth. What is the first shovel for something you want to achieve?

📖 Related Book: \"Red Star Over China\" by Edgar Snow

**📰 Microsoft Announces New Quantum Chip 1,000 Times More Reliable**

📝 Summary: Microsoft announced a breakthrough in quantum computing --- a new chip that is 1,000 times more reliable than its predecessor. Quantum computers can process massive amounts of information simultaneously, solving problems that would take ordinary computers thousands of years. This breakthrough brings us closer to developing new medicines, solving climate change, and designing new materials.

*💬 \"Imagination is more important than knowledge, for knowledge is limited while imagination embraces the entire world.\"*

🤔 Think about: Microsoft\'s scientists failed countless times before achieving this more reliable chip. Would you keep going if it took 1,000 failures for one step forward?

📖 Related Book: \"Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Sea\" by Jules Verne

**📰 India Hit by Extreme Heatwave as UN Warns of Strongest El Nino Yet**

📝 Summary: Parts of India are experiencing a deadly heatwave with temperatures soaring above 47°C. Residents say mornings and nights no longer exist. Meanwhile, the UN warns of the strongest El Nino on record, bringing more extreme weather globally. This is not a sci-fi movie: Earth is sending a fever signal. Every degree of warming reminds us: protecting the planet is about saving ourselves.

*💬 \"Let us not flatter ourselves overmuch on account of our human victories over nature. For each such victory, nature takes its revenge on us.\"*

🤔 Think about: At 47°C, there are people without air conditioning, working under the scorching sun. What is one small thing you could do to help cool down the Earth?

📖 Related Book: \"Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Sea\" by Jules Verne
