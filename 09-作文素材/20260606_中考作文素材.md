  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  📍 中国·全国 \| 🕐 2026年6月6日\                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               **📖 关联名著：《平凡的世界》**\
  \                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              ✍️ 作者：路遥\
  📝 素材提炼：在生态环境保护领域，中国各地持续推进生态文明建设，坚持"绿水青山就是金山银山"的理念。从沙漠治理到城市绿化，从长江禁渔到双碳目标，一代代人像愚公移山一样，用"咬定青山不放松"的韧劲，让天更蓝、山更绿、水更清。守护家园，需要的不是一时冲动，而是日复一日的坚持。\   \
  \                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              💡 案例：塞罕坝三代造林人，用60年时间在荒漠上造出112万亩人工林海，让沙漠变绿洲。这正是"咬定青山不放松"的真实写照。\

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 *✨ 「咬定青山不放松，立根原在破岩中。千磨万击还坚劲，任尔东西南北风。」*\
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 *Biting the green mountain, never letting go, its roots deep in broken rock. After thousands of blows it still stands firm.*\
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 \
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 🤔 思考：你有没有一件坚持了很久的事情？是什么让你坚持下来的？当你想放弃的时候，想一想竹子"咬定青山"的韧劲，你会怎么做？

**🔥 AI时代，为什么文科商科依然重要**

  -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  📍 中国·北京 \| 🕐 2026年6月6日\                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             **📖 关联名著：《朝花夕拾》**\
  \                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            ✍️ 作者：鲁迅\
  📝 素材提炼：随着人工智能飞速发展，很多人问：学文科还有用吗？答案是肯定的。AI能写代码、算数据，但它不懂人心、没有同理心、做不出有温度的决定。真正的创新需要人文关怀和伦理判断。学好语文、历史、哲学，不是在学"没用的知识"，而是在培养AI永远学不会的能力------思考和感受。\   \
  \                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            💡 案例：鲁迅弃医从文，认识到医治国人的精神比医治身体更重要。这正是文科的力量------它直指人心，塑造灵魂。在AI时代，这种能力反而更加珍贵。\
  **🏷️ 考点关键词：创新、选择、科技与人文、思辨**                                                                                                                                                                                                                              \
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               *✨ 「世上本没有路，走的人多了，也便成了路。」*\
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               *There is no road in the world; when many people walk on it, it becomes a road.*\
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               \
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               🤔 思考：你觉得AI能代替语文老师吗？为什么人类需要学会思考，而不只是学会"算"？人和机器最大的区别是什么？

**🌍 English Version**

**📰 Ecological Improvement Requires Persistence like Bamboo**

📝 Summary: China continues advancing ecological civilization, promoting that lucid waters and lush mountains are invaluable assets. From desert control to urban greening, generations show persistence in protecting the environment.

*💬 \"Biting the green mountain, never letting go, its roots deep in broken rock. After thousands of blows it still stands firm.\"*

🤔 Think about: Is there something you have persisted in for a long time? What kept you going? When you feel like giving up, think of the bamboo resilience.

📖 Related Book: \"The Ordinary World\" by Lu Yao

**📰 Why Liberal Arts Matter in the Age of AI**

📝 Summary: As AI develops rapidly, many ask: is studying humanities still useful? The answer is yes. AI can write code and calculate data, but it cannot understand human hearts or show empathy. True innovation requires humanistic care.

*💬 \"There is no road in the world; when many people walk on it, it becomes a road.\"*

🤔 Think about: Do you think AI can replace Chinese teachers? Why do humans need to learn to think, not just to compute? What is the biggest difference between humans and machines?

📖 Related Book: \"Dawn Blossoms Plucked at Dusk\" by Lu Xun

**📰 A Heartwarming Return in High Temperature, Huaibei**

📝 Summary: In Suixi County, Huaibei, Anhui, an elderly person lost important items in scorching heat. A kind person found and returned them. A small act of kindness reveals the warmth of society.

*💬 \"Do not fail to do a good deed because it is small; do not do an evil deed because it is small.\"*

🤔 Think about: Have you been touched by a stranger kindness? If you found someones valuable item, what would you do? Why can small kindness most touch our hearts?

📖 Related Book: \"Rickshaw Boy\" by Lao She

**📰 Police Officers Help Ensure Smooth College Entrance Exam**

📝 Summary: Tomorrow marks the 2026 National College Entrance Exam. Police officers nationwide patrol around exam sites, directing traffic and providing emergency services. Behind every examinee is the support of countless people.

*💬 \"Study for the rise of China.\"*

🤔 Think about: Gaokao is not just an exam but a rite of passage. What does striving mean to a person? What is worth struggling for in your heart?

📖 Related Book: \"Red Star Over China\" by Edgar Snow

**📰 Everest Guide Survives Six-Day Ordeal on Chocolate and Ice**

📝 Summary: Dawa Sherpa, a 57-year-old Nepali guide, ran out of oxygen descending Mount Everest and was trapped in a crevasse at 7,500m for six days. His family began preparing funeral rites. He survived by eating chocolates and chewing ice. He told BBC: I thought I would not make it, but I told myself to hold on.

*💬 \"The most precious thing for a person is life. One should live so that when looking back, one does not regret wasted years nor feel shame for idleness.\"*

🤔 Think about: When Dawa was chewing ice in the crevasse telling himself to hold on, what gave him strength? Have you experienced a darkest before dawn moment?

📖 Related Book: \"How the Steel Was Tempered\" by Nikolai Ostrovsky

**📰 ISS Air Leak Crisis: Astronauts Take Shelter, Successful Repair**

📝 Summary: The ISS suddenly developed a serious air leak. Five astronauts took shelter in the docked SpaceX Dragon while two Russian cosmonauts attempted repairs. Despite different approaches, NASA and Roscosmos shared the same goal: crew safety. The leak was successfully repaired.

*💬 \"Above and below, the four directions are called Yu; from ancient times to today is called Zhou.\"*

🤔 Think about: On the ISS, astronauts from different countries must trust and cooperate. Why is cooperation better than going it alone? Have you faced challenges in teamwork?

📖 Related Book: \"Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Sea\" by Jules Verne
