**📰 Xi Jinping: Pursue Solid Growth Without \'Water Content\'**

📝 Summary: At the Central Economic Work Conference, President Xi Jinping emphasized that all plans must be realistic and pursue solid, high-quality growth without inflated figures. He noted that performance evaluation should focus on real benefits, quality, sustainability, and most importantly, the people\'s sense of gain, happiness, and security.

*💬 \"As Heaven\'s movement is ever vigorous, so must a gentleman ceaselessly strive for self-improvement. As Earth\'s response is solid and steady, so must a gentleman cultivate virtue to carry the world.\"*

🤔 Think about: In your study and life, what counts as \'solid\' effort without \'water content\'? How can you distinguish genuine progress from superficially \'good-looking\' results?

📖 Related Book: \"The Ordinary World\" by Lu Yao

**📰 Ministry of Commerce Opposes US Trade Restrictions Over \'Forced Labor\' Claims**

📝 Summary: Chinese Ministry of Commerce spokesperson He Yongqian stated at a regular press conference that China opposes all forms of unilateral restrictive measures, including trade restrictions imposed under the pretext of \'forced labor\', urging the US to work with China to maintain stable bilateral trade relations.

*💬 \"Study for the rise of China.\"*

🤔 Think about: Why is mutual understanding and respect so important in international relations? If you were a diplomat, how would you explain China to the world?

📖 Related Book: \"Red Star Over China\" by Edgar Snow

**📰 Wuzhen Light: Computing Power in 1 Second Equals 1.4 Billion People Working for 4 Years**

📝 Summary: The \'Wuzhen Light\' supercomputing center in Zhejiang demonstrates China\'s new heights in computing power --- one second of computation equals the work of 1.4 billion people calculating simultaneously for 4 years. This supercomputer plays a key role in weather forecasting, drug development, and AI training, showcasing China\'s technological innovation capabilities.

*💬 \"If you can improve yourself in a day, do so each day, and let there be daily improvement.\"*

🤔 Think about: Rapid technological development is changing our lives. What do you think will be the biggest impact of computing power on humanity\'s future? Technology is a double-edged sword --- what concerns should we watch out for?

📖 Related Book: \"Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Sea\" by Jules Verne

**📰 China Lowers Fuel Prices This Evening, Saving 20 Yuan Per Tank**

📝 Summary: According to the National Development and Reform Commission, domestic refined oil prices will be lowered this evening, saving about 20.5 yuan per tank of 92-octane gasoline. This is one of the biggest fuel price cuts this year, benefiting ordinary car owners and the logistics industry, reflecting macroeconomic regulation focused on people\'s livelihoods.

*💬 \"Livelihood depends on diligence; with diligence, there is no scarcity.\"*

🤔 Think about: A fuel price cut may seem like a small thing, but it affects everyone\'s life. What economic principles can you see in this news? Why does the government regulate energy prices?

📖 Related Book: \"Rickshaw Boy\" by Lao She

**📰 The World Looks East: 100 Seconds of Busy May Diplomacy**

📝 Summary: CCTV News released a video \'100 Seconds of Busy May Diplomacy\', showcasing China\'s intensive diplomatic activities in May. Multiple heads of state visited China, China actively participated in international multilateral cooperation, proposed global development initiatives, and promoted building a community with a shared future for mankind. \'The world looks East\' has become a popular phrase in international discourse.

*💬 \"Worry before the world worries; rejoice after the world rejoices.\"*

🤔 Think about: Why is \'the world looking East\'? What role do you think China should play on the international stage? On a personal level, how do you understand \'a community with a shared future for mankind\'?

📖 Related Book: \"Journey to the West\" by Wu Cheng\'en

**📰 SpaceX Makes History: IPO Priced at \$135, \$1.77T Valuation Shakes Wall Street**

📝 Summary: SpaceX announced its IPO at \$135 per share, with a valuation of \$1.77 trillion, making it the largest IPO in history. Elon Musk broke Wall Street conventions by announcing the price before roadshows and planning to allocate 30% of shares to retail investors. With \$18.67 billion revenue in 2025 spanning aerospace, telecom, and defense, SpaceX is seen as a milestone in commercial space exploration.

*💬 \"Mankind will never stop on the path of exploration. Our journey is the sea of stars.\"*

🤔 Think about: Musk invests his fortune in space exploration. Some call it wasteful, others call it essential for human progress. What do you think? If your dream seems \'unrealistic\' to others, would you still pursue it?

📖 Related Book: \"Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Sea\" by Jules Verne

**📰 US-Iran Agreement May Be Reached This Weekend, Hope for Middle East Peace**

📝 Summary: According to multiple media reports, US President Trump stated that an agreement between the US and Iran could be reached this weekend. Iran\'s Supreme Leader Khamenei made a rare written statement calling for unity. Meanwhile, the US House of Representatives passed its first resolution calling for an end to hostilities against Iran, raising hopes for peace in the Middle East.

*💬 \"Peace is not the absence of conflict, but the ability to find strength to move forward together amidst differences.\"*

🤔 Think about: Why is peace so hard to achieve? If you were a mediator between two conflicting classes, what would you do to reconcile them?

📖 Related Book: \"How the Steel Was Tempered\" by Nikolai Ostrovsky
