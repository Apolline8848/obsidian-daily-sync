**📰 Father of China\'s Jian-8 Fighter Jet, Academician Gu Songfen, Passes Away at 96**

📝 Summary: On June 1, 2026, renowned Chinese aircraft designer and academician Gu Songfen, known as the \'Father of the Jian-8 fighter jet,\' passed away at age 96. He dedicated his entire life to China\'s aviation industry, leading the design of the country\'s first high-altitude, high-speed fighter jet, the Jian-8, and devoting decades of silent dedication to national defense.

*💬 \"Man\'s dearest possession is life. It is given to him but once, and he must live it so as to feel no torturing regrets for wasted years.\"*

🤔 Think about: Academician Gu Songfen spent his whole life building one fighter jet --- how much faith does that require? If you could choose one thing worth pursuing for a lifetime, what would it be?

📖 Related Book: \"How the Steel Was Tempered\" by Nikolai Ostrovsky

From gaming graphics to AI computing, and now PC chips, Nvidia keeps pushing its own boundaries.

*💬 \"If you can renew yourself for one day, then you can do it every day, and keep doing it day after day.\"*

**📰 New Pill Doubles Survival Time for Pancreatic Cancer Patients, Hailed as Breakthrough**

📝 Summary: A new drug called daraxonrasib has doubled the survival time for pancreatic cancer patients, BBC reports. Pancreatic cancer, known as the \'king of cancers,\' is one of the deadliest. *💬 \"The way ahead is long and winding, but I will search high and low to find it.\"*
