**🌍 English Version**

**📰 Xi Jinping: Protect, Inherit and Spread China\'s Cultural Treasures**

📝 Summary: President Xi Jinping emphasized that excellent traditional Chinese culture is the spiritual lifeline of the Chinese nation. He called for protecting, inheriting and spreading these cultural treasures, letting Chinese civilization shine with new brilliance in the new era.

*💬 \"When culture thrives, the nation thrives. When culture is strong, the nation is strong.\"*

🤔 Think about: What traditional culture around you is disappearing? If you were a \'cultural inheritor\', what would you most want to protect and spread?

📖 Related Book: \"Dawn Blossoms Plucked at Dusk\" by Lu Xun

**📰 600+ Sunflowers Bloom on School Rooftop: Teachers and Students Create \'Sky Garden\' for Gaokao Wishes**

📝 Summary: With Gaokao approaching, a rooftop at Gezhi High School in Fuzhou has turned into a golden sea --- over 600 sunflowers cultivated by teachers and students over 80 days are now in full bloom, bringing hope and blessings to students preparing for the college entrance exam.

*💬 \"The sunflower tells me: as long as you face the sun and strive upward, life will become simple and beautiful.\"*

🤔 Think about: If you were to give yourself a gift as you strive forward, would you plant a sunflower or a cactus? Why?

📖 Related Book: \"The Ordinary World\" by Lu Yao

**📰 Cleaner Finds Two Gold Bars Worth Over 200,000 Yuan in Student Dormitory Trash and Returns Them**

📝 Summary: At Tsinghua University\'s graduate campus in Shenzhen, cleaner Liao Youxiang found two gold bars (100g each, worth over 200,000 yuan) while emptying trash. Without hesitation, she turned them in and they were returned to the owner. Netizens praised: \'What shines isn\'t the gold, but her golden heart!\'

*💬 \"A gentleman loves wealth but gets it in the right way.\"*

🤔 Think about: If you found a wallet full of cash and an ID on the street, what would you do? Which is harder --- being honest or being smart?

📖 Related Book: \"Rickshaw Boy\" by Lao She

**📰 UN Warns: Strong El Niño Approaching --- Could Be the Strongest in Decades**

📝 Summary: The UN weather agency has issued an urgent warning: a powerful El Niño is brewing in the Pacific, expected to be the strongest in decades. This means extreme heat, drought and floods across the globe. In parts of India, temperatures have already hit 47°C, with locals saying \'mornings and nights no longer exist.\' Our planet is running a fever.

*💬 \"Let us not too soon triumph over nature\'s victories, for each such victory exacts its revenge.\"*

🤔 Think about: Some say \'protecting the environment is for adults.\' Do you agree? Can you name three eco-friendly actions that middle school students can take?

📖 Related Book: \"Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Sea\" by Jules Verne

**📰 Tom Hanks on Toy Story 5: It Explores the \'Terror\' of Children\'s Screen Addiction**

📝 Summary: Toy Story 5 is set to release, and Tom Hanks reveals a thought-provoking theme: screen dependency. The film shows the anxiety of toys being \'abandoned\' as children become addicted to phones. Buzz Lightyear\'s new enemy isn\'t an evil villain --- it\'s the pair of eyes staring at a screen, no longer looking at toys.

*💬 \"The most precious thing for man is life, which he has only once. A man should live his life so that when he recalls the past, he will not regret his wasted years\...\"*

🤔 Think about: If you converted your daily screen time into \'life battery\', how much charge would you have left? Can you take the \'reduce screen time by 1 hour per day\' challenge for a week?

📖 Related Book: \"How the Steel Was Tempered\" by Nikolai Ostrovsky
