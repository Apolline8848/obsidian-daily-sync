**📰 Shenzhou-23 Crew Begins Space Journey - First Hong Kong Female Astronaut**

📝 Summary: The Shenzhou-23 crew is carrying out a year-long mission on China\'s space station. Li Jiaying, the first Hong Kong female astronaut, will operate the Tianyun Camera for global greenhouse gas monitoring. Astronauts are also growing rice in space. From Yang Liwei\'s 21-hour flight in 2003 to today\'s year-long missions, China\'s space dream keeps reaching higher.

*💬 \"The road ahead is long and winding, I will search high and low.\"*

🤔 Think about: From 21 hours to a full year - it took Chinese astronauts 23 years. What is something you have been persisting in? How has it changed you?

📖 Related Book: \"Journey to the West\" by Wu Cheng\'en

**📰 2026 Gaokao Begins - 13.42 Million Students Take the Exam**

📝 Summary: Today about 13.42 million students across China begin the National College Entrance Exam (Gaokao). It is a special coming-of-age ceremony after 12 years of hard work. Outside exam halls parents wait with hope. Inside young dreams take shape on paper. Gaokao is not the end - it is the beginning of a new journey.

*💬 \"A sharp sword is honed from grinding; a plum blossom fragrance comes from bitter cold.\"*

🤔 Think about: If you could describe Gaokao in one word what would it be? Fairness Struggle Pressure or Hope? Why?

📖 Related Book: \"The Ordinary World\" by Lu Yao

**📰 World Ocean Day: Charting a Path to Maritime Strength with Chinese Characteristics**

📝 Summary: June 8 is UN World Ocean Day. China reflects on its journey toward becoming a maritime power - from the Fishing Port of Fuzhou vision to deep-sea submersibles like Jiaolong and drilling vessel Mengxiang. President Xi reminds us: The ocean is not something to conquer but a home for all life a cradle of life. Development must not come at the cost of the environment.

*💬 \"The sea is vast and open-minded, able to embrace all rivers and all living things.\"*

🤔 Think about: If the ocean could speak what do you think it would say to humans? What small things can we each do to protect it?

📖 Related Book: \"Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Sea\" by Jules Verne

**📰 Pope Leo XIV Visits Spain, Calls for End to Polarization and Embrace of Peace**

📝 Summary: Pope Leo XIV arrived in Spain for his first major European trip the first papal visit in 15 years. Amid Spains political polarization he said: The world is crying out for peace - unfortunately peace strikes some as naive. He urged young people to change history with love and visited migrant communities. In an age of division his message reminds us that understanding is stronger than conflict.

*💬 \"All within the four seas are brothers.\"*

🤔 Think about: When a friend disagrees with you do you argue or listen? Is understanding more important than winning?

📖 Related Book: \"How the Steel Was Tempered\" by Nikolai Ostrovsky

**📰 Indian Maestro Ilaiyaraaja: 50 Years of Reshaping Music with Melodies of Life**

📝 Summary: Ilaiyaraaja a legendary Indian composer is celebrating 50 years in music. From a poor village boy to a maestro who shaped generations he composed over 7000 songs blending Indian ragas with Western symphonies. BBC says: 50 years on he is still reshaping music. His story proves: true passion can cross the river of time and still shine bright after half a century.

*💬 \"If you keep carving without giving up even metal and stone can be engraved.\"*

🤔 Think about: Is there something you have been doing for years? Imagine doing it for 50 more years what would you become?

📖 Related Book: \"Dawn Blossoms Plucked at Dusk\" by Lu Xun
