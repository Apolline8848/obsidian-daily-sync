**The Road to the Competition**

2026年08月04日 \| 人教新目标初中英语词汇故事

Li Wei was a young **musician** who could play the **piano**, the **violin**, and the **drum**. Every day after school, he would rush to the music room to **practice**.

This **weekend** was special --- the annual school **competition** was only days away. Li Wei would perform a solo piano piece at the grand **show**. He never told **anyone** how nervous he really was. \"Let\'s **talk** after class,\" his teacher said. \"This **activity** is supposed to bring you joy, not stress.\"

But before music, there was homework. Li Wei opened his **textbook** and stared at the **physics** equations. **Chemistry** was even harder. With the final **exam** approaching, he tried to study at maximum **speed**, but his mind kept drifting back to melodies and rhythms.

That night, Li Wei began to feel terrible. A sharp **headache** pounded behind his eyes, his **stomach** churned painfully, and his **neck** felt stiff as iron. By **midnight**, he was burning with **fever**. \"I\'m going **crazy**!\" he groaned into his pillow.

The next morning was even worse. An old **toothache** flared up, and a dry **cough** shook his chest. Even his **foot** seemed to **hurt** for no reason. His mother frowned. \"You need a **break**,\" she said. \"We must be **patient** with our bodies.\"

His father had a better idea. \"Let\'s take the **bicycle** and ride out to the **country**. The **weather** is cool --- a little **cloudy** and **windy**, but perfect for fresh air.\" The road stretched **between** golden wheat fields and green meadows.

The countryside lay to the **north** of the city, but the trail also curved **south** around a small hill. To the **east**, the sun was climbing higher; to the **west**, gentle shadows lingered. The ride was about eight **kilometer**, leading toward a small **mountain** in the distance.

They pedaled through a quiet **forest** where every **animal** seemed to be waking up. Soon they reached a clear blue **lake** --- so **pretty** that Li Wei caught his breath. A **large** swan glided across the water, followed by three **cute** baby swans.

Li Wei sat on the sandy **beach** of the lake, closed his eyes, and began to truly **listen** --- to the wind, the water, the distant birds. A small bird nearby started to **sing**. \"I **miss** this feeling,\" he whispered. \"Being calm.\" His shoes got a little **wet** from the gentle waves.

Back in the city, Li Wei took the **subway** home. In just one **minute**, the doors closed and the **train** sped through the dark tunnel. \"I don\'t want to go **anywhere** else,\" he thought. \"That was the best **holiday** I\'ve ever had.\" Through the window, tall **building** flashed by.

That evening, Li Wei had a warm **conversation** with his mother. \"Read this poem **aloud** for me,\" she said. He did, carefully shaping each sound. \"Your **pronunciation** is so clear,\" she smiled. He read every **sentence** with deep feeling. Outside, the old clock **tower** chimed eight times.

Feeling refreshed, Li Wei went to the morning market where a friendly **trader** sold fresh ingredients. He was **hungry** but he **dislike** bargaining over prices. Still, he bought everything without any **trouble** and headed home to **prepare** a special meal for his family.

On the bus ride back, an elderly **passenger** stumbled and nearly fell. His bag **hit** the railing hard, causing a small cut with a trace of **blood**. Li Wei rushed to help, steadying the man until he was safely seated.

**中文译文**

李伟是一个年轻的音乐家，会弹钢琴、拉小提琴、打鼓。每天放学后，他都会冲进音乐室练习。

这个周末很特别------一年一度的学校比赛只有几天了。李伟将在盛大的演出中表演钢琴独奏。他从没告诉过任何人他有多紧张。\"下课后我们谈谈，\"他的老师说。\"这项活动应该带给你快乐，而不是压力。\"

但在音乐之前还有作业。李伟翻开教科书，盯着物理方程式。化学更难。期末考试临近了，他试图以最快速度学习，但思绪总是不由自主地飘向旋律和节奏。

那天晚上，李伟开始感到很不舒服。剧烈的头痛在他眼眶后跳动，胃阵阵翻腾，脖子像铁一样僵硬。到了午夜，他烧得滚烫。\"我要疯了！\"他对着枕头呻吟。

第二天早上更糟。旧牙痛又发作了，干咳震动着他的胸口。连脚都莫名地疼起来。妈妈皱起眉。\"你需要休息，\"她说。\"我们必须对身体有耐心。\"

爸爸有个更好的主意。\"我们骑自行车去乡下。天气凉爽------有点多云有风，但正好适合呼吸新鲜空气。\"道路在金黄的麦田和绿色的草地之间延伸。

乡间位于城市的北面，但小路也向南绕过一个山丘。东方，太阳越升越高；西方，柔和的影子还留连在山谷里。骑了大约八公里，远处出现了一座小山。

他们穿过一片安静的森林，那里的每一种动物似乎都在醒来。很快他们到达了一个清澈的蓝色湖泊------美得让李伟屏住了呼吸。一只大天鹅滑过水面，后面跟着三只可爱的小天鹅。

李伟坐在湖边的沙滩上，闭上眼睛，开始真正地倾听------听风声、水声、远处的鸟鸣。附近一只小鸟开始歌唱。\"我想念这种感觉，\"他低声说。\"平静的感觉。\"他的鞋被轻轻的波浪弄湿了一点。

回到城市，李伟坐地铁回家。仅仅一分钟，门就关上了，火车在黑暗的隧道里飞驰。\"我哪儿也不想去，\"他想。\"这是我度过的最好的假日。\"窗外，高大的建筑物一闪而过。

那天晚上，李伟和妈妈进行了一次温暖的交谈。\"大声给我读这首诗，\"她说。他照做了，仔细地发出每个音。\"你的发音真清晰，\"她笑了。他带着深深的情感读完每一个句子。窗外，古老的钟楼敲了八下。

精神焕发的李伟去了早市，那里有一个友好的摊贩在卖新鲜食材。他很饿，但不喜欢讨价还价。不过，他还是顺利买齐了所有东西，没有任何麻烦，回家为家人准备了一顿特别的饭菜。

在回家的公交车上，一位年迈的乘客踉跄了一下，差点摔倒。他的包重重地撞在栏杆上，碰出了一点血。李伟赶紧上前帮忙，扶着老人直到他安全坐好。
