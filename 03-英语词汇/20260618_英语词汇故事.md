**An Exciting School Sports Day**

2026年06月18日 \| 人教新目标初中英语词汇故事

Last **Thursday**, something **unusual** happened at our school. A big **event** --- the **annual** **sports** **meet** --- **began** with a grand opening. **Colorful** **flags** were **waving** in the **wind**. All the **players** **marched** **onto** the **field**. The **audience** **cheered** **loudly**. **Though** it was just the start, everyone felt excited.

The first **race** was the **hundred**-**meter** dash. The **runners** **stood** at the **starting** **line**. A **signal** **shot** rang out, and they ran **quickly** **forward** to **compete**. **Several** **minutes** later, a new school **record** was **broke**! The **crowd** **suddenly** jumped up, shouting with joy.

**Meanwhile**, at the **high** **jump** **area**, another **athlete** **attempted** to **clear** the **bar**. A heavy **weight** was **threw** even **farther** than the **previous** throw. The **winner** was **congratulated** by his **teammate**, who simply **nodded** with a **real** smile.

The **relay** race was the **final** event. **Each** **team** **member** **waited** **nervously**. The **stick** was **passed** quickly **from** one runner to another. With great **determination**, our class **won** first **prize**! This **valuable** **lesson** taught us that **effort** leads to success.

**中文译文**

上周四，我们学校发生了一件不寻常的事。一个大型活动------年度运动会------在盛大的开幕式中开始了。五彩的旗帜在风中飘扬。所有运动员列队走进运动场。观众们大声欢呼。虽然这只是开始，但每个人都感到兴奋。

第一项比赛是百米短跑。选手们站在起跑线上。一声信号枪响起，他们快速向前奔跑，进行比赛。几分钟后，一项新的学校纪录被打破了！人群突然跳了起来，欢呼雀跃。

与此同时，在跳高区域，另一名运动员试图越过横杆。一个沉重的铅球被投得比之前更远。获胜者得到了队友的祝贺，队友只是点了点头，露出了真诚的微笑。

接力赛是最后一个项目。每个队的队员都紧张地等待着。接力棒迅速从一个选手传递到另一个。凭着巨大的决心，我们班赢得了一等奖！这宝贵的教训告诉我们：努力带来成功。
