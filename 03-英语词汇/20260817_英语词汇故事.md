**The Lost Puppy on a Rainy Day**

2026年08月17日 \| 人教新目标（Go for It!）初中英语词汇故事

**一、英文故事**

On a **cloudy** Saturday morning, the **weather** grew **windy** and cold, and soon it began to **rain**. I had planned to **exercise** in the **park**, but the **problem** was that the sky might turn to **snow** before noon.

We **usually** **leave** home **early** on weekends, but this time Mom said we could go **outside** for only one **minute**. As we **walk** near the old **bridge**, we **listen** carefully and hear a tiny cry.

Under a large stone we find a small puppy. He is **cute** and **smart**, but his leg is **hurt**, so he cannot **run**. He is **alone**, shivering in the cold, and one little paw looks **broken**.

We carry the puppy home and **clean** the mud off his fur. Mom says the most **important** thing is to keep him warm, so she heats some **soup** and gives him a **delicious** piece of bread.

Ben finds a **message** on the puppy\'s collar. It says the dog belongs to a girl in a village about one **hundred** meters away --- not **far** at all. We decide to **search** for her **during** the afternoon.

As the storm grows heavier, Dad says we can **drive** there by car. I want to **ride** my bike, but Dad says the road is too wet. We **never** give up on the little dog, because we want to **save** him and return him to a warm, **friendly** family.

Halfway there, something **strange** and **wonderful** begins to **happen**: a lady in a red coat calls our names in a warm **voice**. She is the girl\'s mother! She says the puppy slipped out when the gate swung open, and she has looked for him for **several** hours.

We **explain** that we found the puppy under a stone. The lady thanks us and says our **kindness** is something she will not **forget**. She says we can visit him **again** any time, and that will **change** everything for us.

At the girl\'s house, the **whole** family gathers around the puppy. The girl\'s mother tells us he is very **clever**, and that is how the **matter** began --- he learned to open the gate. She asks if we can help **fix** it, and although we meet some **difficulty**, we **finally** make the latch close properly. She reminds us to be **careful** with the tools.

Then a loud thunder makes the puppy jump, and he **suddenly** hides under a chair. We smile and say he does not need to be afraid, because his family will not **allow** any harm to come to him. He should feel safe **instead** of being scared, and he seems to **understand** our words.

The girl says she will **hope** for sunny days, and she asks if we **believe** the sky will clear soon. I tell her it is very **possible**, because the clouds are already moving. We also **solve** the riddle of the gate, and we **trust** that the puppy will stay safe now.

Before we go, the mother shares a little **knowledge** with us about how to care for dogs. She goes on to **suggest** that we visit often, and she speaks so **politely** that we feel proud. She points in the **direction** of our home and thanks us for the **effort** we made today.

She says our help was truly **worth** it, and her kind words **support** us all the way home. As we go, the puppy gives a happy little bark, as if to **warn** us to come back soon. That special day taught us that caring for a small friend is one of the best things in the world.

**二、中文译文**

在一个多云的星期六早晨，天气变得风大又寒冷，不久就下起雨来。我本打算去公园锻炼，但问题是到中午天空可能会下雪。

我们通常在周末很早就离开家，但这一次妈妈说我们只能到外面待一分钟。当我们走到那座旧桥附近时，我们仔细地听，听到了一声微弱的叫声。

在一块大石头下面，我们发现了一只小狗。它又可爱又聪明，但它的腿受了伤，所以它跑不动。它独自待在那里，在寒冷中发抖，一只小爪子看起来已经断了。

我们把小狗抱回家，清理掉它毛上的泥。妈妈说最重要的事情是让它暖和起来，于是她热了一些汤，还给了它一片美味的面包。

本在小狗的项圈上发现了一条留言。上面说这只狗属于一个住在村里、离这里大约一百米远的女孩------一点也不远。我们决定下午去找她。

随着暴风雨越来越大，爸爸说我们可以开车去那里。我想骑我的自行车，但爸爸说路太湿了。我们从不放弃这只小狗，因为我们想救它，把它送回到一个温暖友好的家庭。

走到半路，一件既奇怪又奇妙的事情开始发生了：一位穿红大衣的女士用温暖的声音喊出了我们的名字。她就是那个女孩的妈妈！她说小狗是在大门敞开时溜出去的，她已经找它好几个小时了。

我们解释说，我们是在一块石头下面发现小狗的。女士感谢了我们，并说我们的善良是她永远不会忘记的。她说我们随时都可以再来看它，而这将改变我们的一切。

在女孩家里，全家人都围着小狗聚在一起。女孩的妈妈告诉我们，它非常聪明，整件事就是这样开始的------它学会了打开大门。她问我们能否帮忙修好它，虽然我们遇到了一些困难，但我们终于把门闩弄好了。她提醒我们使用工具时要小心。

这时一声响雷让小狗跳了起来，它突然躲到了椅子下面。我们笑着说它不用害怕，因为它的家人不会允许任何伤害降临到它身上。我们告诉它，它应该感到安全而不是害怕，它似乎听懂了我们的意思。

女孩说她希望天气早日放晴，并问我们是否相信天空很快就会转晴。我告诉她这很有可能，因为云已经在移动了。我们还解开了大门的谜团，并且相信小狗现在会平安无事了。

在我们离开之前，妈妈和我们分享了一些关于如何照顾狗的小知识。她接着建议我们常来玩，而且她说话非常有礼貌，让我们感到很自豪。她指了我们家的方向，并感谢我们今天付出的努力。

她说我们的帮助真的非常值得，她温暖的话语一路支持着我们回到家。当我们离开时，小狗快乐地叫了一声，仿佛在提醒我们要早点回来。那个特别的日子教会了我们，关爱一个小小的朋友是世界上最好的事情之一。
