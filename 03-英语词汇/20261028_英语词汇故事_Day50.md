**Day 50: Lily\'s Busy Sunday**

英语词汇小故事 · 第50天（70词）

**第一部分 · 英文故事**

On Sunday morning, Lily finished her homework at the kitchen table. Her **dad** read the newspaper while **mommy** cooked breakfast. First she practised **division** and **subtraction**, checking every answer twice. \"Watch your **comma** and your **punctuation**,\" Dad said with gentle **humour**. She opened her textbook and read a long **appendix** about how Marie Curie discovered **radium**. She loved **biochemistry** because it explained the chemistry of every living **being**. A tiny **dot** on the page marked a **parallel** line. Her new **thinking** felt clear and calm.

After lunch they went to the sports centre, a bright and **smoke-free** hall. Lily tried **bowling** and laughed when she missed the pins. She watched a fast game of **rugby** and a short **boxing** match. A friendly **recordholder** showed her his medal, and she used a **stopwatch** to time her **running** around the track. In a quiet corner, people practised **shooting** at targets. An **English-speaking** coach taught her some **spoken** phrases. \"Practice every day,\" he said, **regardless** of how hard it feels.

Then they went **shopping** at a large **self-service** store. A busy **businesswoman** paid by **cheque**, and a kind **madam** helped Mommy choose a red **miniskirt**. They weighed flour, **gram** by gram. Lily kept an old **franc** coin in her pocket. Near the door, a **chairwoman** talked about **politics**, and a thoughtful **communist** teacher explained the history of **communism**. \"An honest **statesman** can improve a whole **system**,\" she said. **Overhead**, a speaker played soft **pop** music.

On the way home they passed a small **pub**, and a **smoker** stood outside with a **cigarette** of **tobacco**. Dad reminded Lily never to try **smoking** or **alcohol**. A **pregnant** woman smiled at them. In the park, a **mosquito** buzzed near an **ox** that pulled a cart. A little path began to **wind** through the grass, and Lily met an old **playmate** there. They remembered a wise **saying**: \"Slow and steady wins the race.\" Her friend was **sceptical** at first but soon agreed. They used the **lavatory** and climbed through a small **opening** in the fence.

Back home, Lily printed her story on a new **printer**. The old **modem** blinked and the **disk** spun, but the **printing** was slow. Dad fixed the shelf with a **stainless** screw, and the **output** was neat and clear. He still needed to finish some **repairs** on the door. Lily thanked **god** for the happy day. She drew with a **ballpoint** pen while her **right-handed** brother wrote a note. The **so-so** weather did not matter. On television, a **golf** match played, and Lily dreamed she was a little **superman**. People called the old farmer **simple-minded**, but Lily found him wise and kind.

**第二部分 · 中文译文**

星期天早上，莉莉在餐桌旁完成了作业。她的爸爸在看报纸，妈妈在做早餐。她先练习除法和减法，每道题都检查两遍。「注意你的逗号和标点符号。」爸爸温和而幽默地说。她翻开课本，读了一篇很长的附录，讲的是居里夫人如何发现了镭。她喜欢生物化学，因为它解释了一切生物体内的化学变化。页面上一个小圆点标出一条平行线。她新的思考变得清晰而平静。

午饭后，他们去了体育中心，那是一个明亮而无烟的大厅。莉莉试了保龄球，没打中球瓶时笑了起来。她观看了一场快速的橄榄球赛和一场短暂的拳击比赛。一位友好的纪录保持者给她看了他的奖牌，她用秒表给自己绕跑道跑步计时。在一个安静的角落里，人们对着靶子练习射击。一位说英语的教练教了她几句口语。「每天都要练习。」他说，无论多难都要坚持。

然后他们去一家大型自助商店购物。一位忙碌的女商人用支票付款，一位和蔼的女士帮妈妈挑了一条红色的超短裙。他们称面粉，一克一克地称。莉莉口袋里还留着一枚旧法郎硬币。在门口附近，一位女主席谈论着政治，一位深思的共产主义者老师讲解了共产主义的历史。「一位诚实的政治家可以改善整个体制。」她说。头顶上方，一个扬声器播放着轻柔的流行音乐。

回家的路上，他们经过一家小酒吧，一位吸烟者站在外面，抽着一支烟草做的香烟。爸爸提醒莉莉永远不要尝试吸烟或喝酒。一位怀孕的女士朝他们微笑。在公园里，一只蚊子在拉车的公牛旁边嗡嗡叫。一条小路开始蜿蜒穿过草地，莉莉在那儿遇到了一位老玩伴。他们想起一句谚语：「慢而稳，赢得比赛。」她的朋友起初很怀疑，但很快就同意了。他们去了洗手间，然后从篱笆上的一个小口子钻了过去。

回到家，莉莉用一台新打印机打印了她的故事。旧调制解调器在闪烁，磁盘在转动，但打印速度很慢。爸爸用一颗不锈钢螺丝修好了架子，打印出来的成品整洁清晰。他还要把门上的修理活儿做完。莉莉感谢上帝赐予这快乐的一天。她用圆珠笔画画，而她惯用右手的弟弟在写便条。天气一般也无所谓。电视上播放着一场高尔夫球赛，莉莉梦想着自己是个小超人。人们都说那位老农头脑简单，但莉莉却觉得他睿智又善良。
