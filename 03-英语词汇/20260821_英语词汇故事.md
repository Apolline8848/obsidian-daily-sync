**一、英文故事**

**The Mountain Village Journey**

Last summer holiday, Li Hua **decided** to make a **difference**. He wanted to be a **volunteer** in a small **village** in the **mountain**. He **left** his home **early** in the morning, took the **subway** to the railway **station**, and got on a **train**. The **weather** was **cloudy** at first, but soon it turned **sunny** and warm.

After two hours, he got off and **rode** a bicycle for three **kilometers**. Then he **crossed** a narrow **bridge** that went **across** the **river**, and finally reached a **pretty** **lake**. The air was fresh, and he could **feel** the cool **windy** breeze. He began to **enjoy** the long **journey**.

There, an old man named Grandpa Wang was the **owner** of a small farm. He lived alone and was sometimes **lonely**, but he was still **strong**. He **noticed** Li Hua and smiled warmly. He **offered** Li Hua a room to stay, and Li Hua **rested** after the long trip.

The next morning, Grandpa Wang had a pain in his **stomach** and a slight **fever**. Li Hua was **nervous**, but he **wrote** down the doctor\'s number and called for help. It did not **matter** --- the doctor arrived in twenty **minutes**. A **passenger** on a bus nearby was also in **trouble**; he had **hurt** his leg.

That afternoon, Li Hua joined the children. They had a small **club** where they liked to play the **guitar**, sing, and **dance**. Some children played **chess**, and Grandpa Wang told them funny **stories**. Li Hua taught the children to **swim** safely and to **climb** the small hill. They had a **wonderful** time **together**.

That evening, Li Hua was **hungry**. Grandpa Wang cooked dinner with eggs, vegetables, and fish. \'My pond has a **hundred** fish, so we have **enough** food,\' he said with a smile. Li Hua promised to **visit** again next year.

On Li Hua\'s last morning, the children held a small **competition**. Li Hua had to **choose** the winner. Every child **hoped** to win, and their parents came to watch. \'This place is **famous** for its brave children,\' an old teacher said with a smile.

When it was over, every child got a small prize. Li Hua was filled with a deep **feeling** of **satisfaction** and **joy**. He **encouraged** the children to study hard and **achieve** their dreams. \'You can **discover** your own **secret** talent if you are **patient** and **active**,\' he said. \'Never stop learning --- **review** your lessons and build your **knowledge**, then use it **wisely** to **create** a better life.\'

**二、中文译文**

去年暑假，李华决定要做出一点改变。他想去山里的小村庄当一名志愿者。他一大早就离开了家，先坐地铁到火车站，然后登上了一列火车。起初天气多云，但很快就变得晴朗而温暖。

两个小时后，他下了车，又骑了三公里自行车。然后他穿过一座横跨河流的窄桥，终于来到一个美丽的湖边。空气清新，他能感受到凉爽的风。他开始享受这段漫长的旅程。

在那里，一位名叫王爷爷的老人是一个小农场的主人。他独自生活，有时很孤独，但仍然很强壮。他注意到了李华，热情地微笑。他给李华提供了一个房间，李华在长途旅行后休息了一下。

第二天早上，王爷爷胃疼，还有点发烧。李华很紧张，但他写下了医生的电话号码并打电话求助。没关系------二十分钟后医生就到了。附近公交车上的一位乘客也遇到了麻烦，他的腿受伤了。

那天下午，李华和孩子们一起玩。他们有一个小社团，喜欢弹吉他、唱歌和跳舞。有些孩子下国际象棋，王爷爷给他们讲有趣的故事。李华教孩子们安全地游泳和爬小山。他们一起度过了一段精彩的时光。

那天晚上，李华饿了。王爷爷做了晚饭，有鸡蛋、蔬菜和鱼。他笑着说：「我的池塘里有一百条鱼，所以我们的食物足够了。」李华答应明年再来。

在李华的最后一天早上，孩子们举行了一场小比赛。李华要选出获胜者。每个孩子都希望能赢，他们的父母都来观看。一位老老师微笑着说：「这个地方以勇敢的孩子而闻名。」

比赛结束后，每个孩子都得到了一份小奖品。李华心中充满了深深的满足感和喜悦。他鼓励孩子们努力学习，实现他们的梦想。他说：「如果你们有耐心、积极主动，就能发现自己的秘密天赋。永远不要停止学习------复习功课，积累知识，然后明智地运用它去创造更美好的生活。」
