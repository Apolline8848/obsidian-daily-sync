**The Science Camp Adventure**

2026年08月03日 \| 人教新目标初中英语词汇故事

**English Story**

Last summer, our class visited a research station by the **sea**. Located in the **western** part of the country, the station was famous for its work on ocean **pollution**. I didn\'t know what to **expect** --- it was my first time traveling so far from home.

We took a **train** early in the morning. Outside the window, a vast green **field** stretched to the horizon. A **worker** was harvesting crops under the sun. Suddenly, a small **mouse** ran across the carriage floor. Lily screamed, thinking it was **ugly**, but Tom just laughed.

When we arrived, the station building looked **fine** --- modest but clean. We climbed the **stair**s to the main entrance, where an **official** greeted us with a warm **expression**. "Welcome, young explorers!" he said.

We were shown to our **room**s on the second floor. A **tent** and camping gear sat in the corner --- we would sleep outdoors one night. A card on the desk showed the camp's **address** and daily schedule.

Our first activity was to **listen** to a visiting **professor** from the national university. "The **universe** is full of mysteries," she said. "Every star, every **ray** of light holds a secret. The **root** of all science is a simple phrase: 'I **guess**... what if?'"

After the lecture, we went outside to **pick** samples near the shore. We collected leaves, **salt** crystals, and shells. I tried to **grasp** a small crab, but it slipped away. Nearby, Tom found an old **machine** half-buried in the sand. It was covered in rust and quite **heavy**.

"This was used by a **pioneer** scientist who studied marine life decades ago," our guide explained. "He was a remarkable **character** --- determined and fearless."

That evening, we had a **picnic** on the beach. We ate sandwiches and **sweet** watermelon, telling stories under the orange sky. Tom told **humorous** jokes that made everyone laugh until our stomachs hurt.

Suddenly, dark clouds rolled in. A fierce **storm** was approaching! The wind was so **strong** that it nearly blew our tents away. We rushed back to the station, my **sock**s soaked through. I hung my wet **overcoat** by the heater.

Inside, the **power** went out, leaving us in darkness. Mr. Chen, our teacher, lit a candle and began to **read** from an old book. The story was about a courageous man who helped **liberate** people from oppression --- a former **slave** who risked everything for freedom.

The next morning, the storm was gone. Everything felt calm --- a deep sense of **peace** filled the air. The **green** trees sparkled with raindrops. It **amaze**d me how quickly nature could change.

"Today we'll **check** the water quality," announced our instructor. Using **soap** and testing **tool**s, we measured pH levels in a nearby stream. "We must all work to **reduce** pollution. The **government** has a **system** of environmental laws, but **each** of us must do our part too."

At noon, a group of **visitor**s arrived --- students from another city. We had a friendly **dialogue** about our experiments. One of the **guest**s, a quiet boy named David, told us he was **unable** to join the full camp --- the fee was too **expensive** for his family. But the camp director, a **reasonable** man, immediately offered him a scholarship.

"That's not **simply** about money," the director said. "Every child deserves a chance to learn. It was the **least** I could do." His words moved everyone deeply.

Inspired, we decided to **open** a small library. We collected books and built shelves from old **wood**. "Who **would** have imagined this?" Lily said with a smile.

On the last day, we visited a historic **hotel** where the station's founder once stayed. Now it was a museum. A **more** experienced guide --- a former government researcher --- shared stories about the early days of marine science in our **republic**.

"You must learn to **avoid** quick judgments," she advised. "Stay **fit** in both body and mind. The **way** of science is long, but every step matters."

Looking at the ocean, I thought about the **past** week. Sometimes memories can feel like a weight, but good moments give us strength. You never know what will **happen** when you try something new.

On the train home, Tom fell asleep on my **shoulder**. He had a peaceful smile. I thought he was dreaming about our next adventure.

**中文译文**

去年夏天，我们班去海边的研究站参观。研究站位于国家的西部，以海洋污染研究而闻名。我不知道会有什么期待------这是我第一次离家这么远。

我们一大早乘火车出发。窗外，一片广阔的绿色田野延伸到地平线。一个工人正在阳光下收割庄稼。突然，一只小老鼠跑过车厢地板。莉莉尖叫起来，觉得它很难看，但汤姆只是笑了笑。

到达后，研究站大楼看起来还不错------简朴但干净。我们爬上楼梯来到正门，一位工作人员带着温暖的表情迎接我们。他说：「欢迎你们，年轻的探索者！」

我们被带到二楼自己的房间。角落里放着帐篷和露营装备------我们将在外面睡一晚。桌上的一张卡片写着营地的地址和日程安排。

我们的第一项活动是聆听一位来自国家大学的教授的讲座。她说：「宇宙充满了奥秘。每一颗星星，每一缕光线都蕴含着秘密。所有科学的根源都是一句简单的话：『我猜......如果呢？』」

讲座结束后，我们到外面采集海岸附近的样本。我们收集了树叶、盐晶体和贝壳。我试图抓住一只小螃蟹，但它溜走了。附近，汤姆在沙子里发现了一台旧机器，锈迹斑斑，相当沉重。

我们的导游解释说：「这是几十年前一位先驱科学家研究海洋生物时用过的。他是一个了不起的人物------意志坚定，无所畏惧。」

那天晚上，我们在沙滩上野餐。我们吃着三明治和甜甜的西瓜，在橙色的天空下讲故事。汤姆讲着幽默的笑话，把大家逗得笑个不停。

突然，乌云密布。一场猛烈的暴风雨来临了！风如此猛烈，差点把我们的帐篷吹走。我们匆忙跑回研究站，我的袜子湿透了。我把湿漉漉的大衣挂在暖炉旁。

室内，电力中断了，我们陷入黑暗。陈老师点亮蜡烛，开始读一本旧书。故事讲述了一个勇敢的人帮助人们从压迫中解放出来------一个曾经的奴隶，为自由冒着一切风险。

第二天早上，暴风雨过去了。一切都平静下来------空气中弥漫着深深的和平感。绿色的树木在雨滴中闪闪发光。大自然的快速变化让我惊叹不已。

指导员宣布：「今天我们要检查水质。」我们用肥皂和测试工具测量了附近溪流的pH值。「我们都必须努力减少污染。政府有一套环境法律体系，但我们每个人都必须尽自己的一份力。」

中午，一群参观者来了------来自另一个城市的学生。我们友好地交流了实验心得。其中一位客人，一个叫大卫的安静男孩，告诉我们他无法参加整个营期------费用对他的家庭来说太贵了。但营地主任，一个通情达理的人，立刻给他提供了奖学金。

主任说：「这不只是钱的问题。每个孩子都值得拥有学习的机会。这是我最起码能做的。」他的话深深打动了每个人。

受到启发，我们决定开设一个小型图书馆。我们收集书籍，用旧木头搭建书架。莉莉笑着说：「谁能想象到这一切呢？」

最后一天，我们参观了一家历史悠久的旅店，研究站创始人曾在那里住过。现在它是一个博物馆。一位更有经验的导游------前政府研究人员------分享了关于我们共和国海洋科学早期历史的故事。

她建议道：「你们必须学会避免草率的判断。保持身心都健康。科学之路很长，但每一步都很重要。」

望着大海，我回想着过去的一周。有时记忆会像重担一样，但美好的时刻会给我们力量。你永远不知道尝试新事物时会发生什么。

在回家的火车上，汤姆靠在我的肩膀上睡着了。他脸上带着平静的微笑。我想他正梦着我们的下一次冒险。
