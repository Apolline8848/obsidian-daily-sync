**A Special Trip to Grandma\'s House**

**一、英文故事**

On a bright **weekend**, my sister Lily and I **leave** our home **early** to visit Grandma. She lives in a quiet little **village**, about one **kilometer** from the old **bridge**. We **usually** take the bus, but this morning we choose to **walk** the whole way and enjoy the fresh air.

Halfway there, we **cross** a small river and see an old man trying to **repair** his bicycle by the road. He looks **worried** because he must go to the market soon. Lily is **brave**, so she steps up to **offer** him our help. In just one **minute**, we fix the chain, and the man smiles and thanks us again and again. He is very **polite**.

Two **hundred** steps later, we reach Grandma\'s house. Her yard is not **far** from the fields, and a **cute** little puppy jumps out to greet us. The puppy is very **smart**; he already knows our smell. Our old cat, however, is still **lazy** and stays asleep on the warm stone.

Grandma is cooking chicken **soup** in the kitchen, and the whole house smells **delicious**. We **bring** a big cake for her birthday, and we **wear** our best coats for the party. Grandma\'s only **rule** is that everyone must be kind, and today everyone follows it.

After lunch, I **exercise** with Grandpa in the garden, and then I **ride** my old bike around the yard. The neighbor\'s children **run** over to play with the puppy, and together we **clean** the front yard until it shines. We **never** feel tired, because helping makes us happy.

In the evening, we **prepare** the birthday dinner together. I want to **improve** my cooking, so Grandma lets me stir the big pot. We **invite** all the neighbors, and they gladly **accept** our invitation, because nobody wants to miss the **surprise**.

When Grandma comes into the room, she looks **serious** for a second, then she smiles so **clearly** that everyone cheers. The party is **fantastic**! We sing and **listen** to Grandma\'s old stories. Later, she tells us a little **secret** from her youth, and we **imagine** her dancing in the fields long ago.

Grandma says the most **important** thing in life is not money but love. These words make me feel **nervous** yet happy. I look **outside** the window and **notice** that the stars are very bright. I **promise** her that I will come back every month, and I will not **forget** this warm night.

The next morning, a kind **stranger** stops by and asks for directions to the bus stop. Grandma is very **patient** and shows him the way. She also gives him some cake and says we should always pay **attention** to those in need. We **volunteer** to wash the dishes and help a **lonely** old neighbor tidy her house. It gives us real **satisfaction** and **joy**.

After that, the neighbor\'s children **encourage** me to join their ball game. I feel a little shy, but I stay **calm** and try my best. I am not very **strong**, but I am **honest** and I do not give up. My parents look **proud** of me, and I can see the **pride** in their eyes. It fills my heart with the true **value** of family.

As we say goodbye, the children give us a small bag of apples, and everyone is so **friendly** that I want to stay longer. We **raise** our hands to wave goodbye. I **wish** Grandma good health and **save** a piece of cake for Dad. We **manage** to catch the last bus, and Dad will **drive** us the rest of the way home.

On the way home, I **discover** a small note in my pocket from Grandma. It says she is **ready** to visit us next month. We **achieve** more than a happy memory; we take home a heart full of love.

**二、中文译文**

在一个晴朗的周末，我和妹妹莉莉早早离开家去看望奶奶。她住在一个安静的小村庄，离那座旧桥大约一公里。我们通常坐公交车，但今天早上我们选择一路步行，享受新鲜空气。

走到半路，我们穿过一条小河，看到一位老人正在路边修理他的自行车。他看起来很担心，因为他必须尽快去市场。莉莉很勇敢，于是她走上前去主动提供我们的帮助。只用了一分钟，我们就修好了链条，老人笑着一遍又一遍地感谢我们。他非常有礼貌。

走了两百步之后，我们到了奶奶家。她的院子离田地不远，一只可爱的小狗跳出来迎接我们。这只小狗很聪明，它已经认识我们的气味。而我们家的老猫却还是很懒，躺在温暖的石头上睡觉。

奶奶正在厨房里煮鸡汤，整栋房子闻起来香喷喷的。我们为她的生日带了一个大蛋糕，还为聚会穿上了最好的外套。奶奶唯一的规矩就是每个人都要善良，而今天大家都遵守了它。

午饭后，我和爷爷在花园里锻炼，然后我骑着旧自行车在院子里转圈。邻居家的孩子们跑过来和小狗一起玩，我们一起把前院打扫得干干净净。我们从不觉得累，因为帮助别人让我们很快乐。

到了晚上，我们一起准备生日晚餐。我想提高自己的厨艺，所以奶奶让我搅拌大锅。我们邀请了所有的邻居，他们都高兴地接受了我们的邀请，因为没有人想错过这个惊喜。

当奶奶走进房间时，她先是看起来一脸严肃，接着便笑得那么明显，大家都欢呼起来。聚会棒极了！我们唱歌，听奶奶讲过去的故事。后来，她告诉我们一个她年轻时的秘密，我们想象着她多年前在田野里跳舞的样子。

奶奶说，人生中最重要的事情不是金钱，而是爱。这些话让我既紧张又开心。我望向窗外，注意到星星非常明亮。我向她承诺每个月都会回来，我绝不会忘记这个温暖的夜晚。

第二天早上，一位和善的陌生人路过，向我们问去公交站的路。奶奶非常有耐心，给他指了路。她还给了他一些蛋糕，并说我们应该始终关注那些需要帮助的人。我们自愿洗碗，并帮助一位孤独的老邻居整理房子。这带给我们真正的满足感和快乐。

之后，邻居的孩子们鼓励我加入他们的球赛。我有点害羞，但我保持冷静，尽我所能。我不是很强壮，但我很诚实，而且我从不放弃。我的父母看起来为我感到自豪，我能看到他们眼中的骄傲。这让我心中充满了家庭的真正价值。

当我们道别时，孩子们送给我们一小袋苹果，每个人都非常友好，让我想多待一会儿。我们举手挥手告别。我祝奶奶身体健康，并给爸爸留了一块蛋糕。我们设法赶上了末班车，爸爸会开车送我们走完剩下的回家的路。

在回家的路上，我在口袋里发现了一张奶奶写的小纸条。上面说她下个月准备好来看望我们。我们收获的不仅仅是一段快乐的记忆；我们把一颗充满爱的心带回了家。
