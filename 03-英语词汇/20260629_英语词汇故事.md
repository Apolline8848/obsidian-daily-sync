**The Mountain Village Journey**

2026年06月29日 \| 人教新目标初中英语词汇故事

Last summer, Tom **decided** to **spend** his school **holiday** as a **volunteer** in a small **village** in the **north** of the **country**. He wanted to **discover** the real **difference** between city life and village life. The long **journey** ahead made him feel a bit **lonely**. He packed his **private** things and **prepared** carefully for the adventure.

He **left** home **early** on a **cloudy** morning and went to the railway **station**. The **train** would **ride** for two **hundred** **kilometer**s through green fields. The trip took about ninety **minute**s. Looking out the window, he **felt** a **secret** **hope** rising inside his heart.

At the stop, he had to **cross** an old stone **bridge** above a wide **river**. Then he needed to **drive** **between** tall **mountain**s along a narrow road. **Across** the valley, tiny houses sparkled under the **sunny** afternoon sky.

When Tom arrived, a **polite** old man --- the **owner** of a small inn --- **showed** him around the village. They **shook** hands, and the man **offered** him a clean room. Tom learned to be **honest** and grateful to everyone here.

The next day, Tom visited the village **club**. Children there loved to **swim** in the lake and **dance** to happy music. A boy played the **guitar** while another beat a small **drum**. Everyone was so **active** and full of life! Tom began to **write** down his experiences. He dreamed he could **create** a storybook one day.

Later, Tom **climbed** a green hill with the children. He taught them about the **value** of **knowledge**. He **encouraged** them to pay **attention** in class. \"You can **achieve** anything,\" he said. A boy quietly showed him a worn **wallet** --- inside was a photo of his late mother.

One afternoon, a **famous** musician came and gave a **wonderful** show. He was a talented **performer** who had won many awards. Tom noticed a **nervous** girl who wanted to join the music **competition**. He helped her **choose** her favorite song.

The girl finally **entered** the stage and sang so bravely. The **result** was amazing! Everyone felt **proud** of her courage. Tom experienced deep **joy**. He realized that being **brave** can make magical things **happen**.

Before Tom left, the children gave him a card with a sweet **message**. Tom promised to **visit** them again someday. He would always **enjoy**. A **sign** at the village gate read, \'Welcome back anytime\' --- a gift that money could never buy.

**中文译文**

去年夏天，汤姆决定把学校假期花在北方乡下的一个小村庄当志愿者。他想要发现城市生活和乡村生活之间的真正差异。即将到来的漫长旅程让他感到有点孤独。他收拾好私人物品，为这次冒险做了精心准备。

一个多云的清晨，他早早地离开家，前往火车站。火车将行驶两百公里，穿过绿色的田野。这趟旅程大约花了九十分钟。望着窗外，他感到内心升起一个隐秘的希望。

到了中转站后，他不得不穿过一条宽阔河流上的一座古老的石桥。然后他需要在巍峨的群山之间沿着一条狭窄的道路驱车前行。山谷对岸，小小的房子在阳光明媚的午后天空下闪闪发光。

当汤姆到达时，一位彬彬有礼的老人------一家小旅馆的主人------带他参观了村庄。他们握了握手，老人为他提供了一间干净的房间。汤姆学会了要对这里的每一个人诚实和感恩。

第二天，汤姆参观了村里的俱乐部。那里的孩子们喜欢在湖里游泳，随着欢快的音乐跳舞。一个男孩弹着吉他，另一个敲着小鼓。每个人都那么活跃、充满活力！汤姆开始写下他的经历。他梦想有一天能创作一本故事书。

后来，汤姆和孩子们一起爬上了一座绿色的小山。他教他们知识的价值。他鼓励他们在课堂上集中注意力。\"你们可以实现任何事情，\"他说。一个男孩悄悄地给他看了一个旧钱包------里面是他已故母亲的照片。

一天下午，一位著名的音乐家来了，带来了一场精彩的表演。他是一位才华横溢的表演者，获得过很多奖项。汤姆注意到一个紧张的女孩想参加音乐比赛。他帮她选择了她最喜欢的歌曲。

女孩终于登上舞台，唱得非常勇敢。结果令人惊叹！每个人都为她的勇气感到骄傲。汤姆感受到深深的喜悦。他意识到，勇敢能让神奇的事情发生。

在汤姆离开之前，孩子们给了他一张卡片，上面写着温馨的留言。汤姆答应有一天会再来看望他们。他会永远享受这段回忆。村口的牌子上写着\"随时欢迎回来\"------一份金钱永远买不到的礼物。
