**The Courage to Play**

**Part 1 英文故事**

Kai\'s **home** was always filled with music. Every day after school he played the **piano** at the community **center**, and he could **dance** to fast songs with his little sister. On Fridays he even beat his best friend at **chess**. But his greatest **dream** was to become a **musician**, just like his grandpa.

One morning, his teacher Ms. Lin announced, \'Our school will hold a talent **show** next month. I want you to perform!\' Kai was **excited** but nervous. He could **speak** English well and **also** sing, yet performing alone was different. He felt **afraid** that he would hit wrong notes in front of so many **people**.

On the weekend, Kai took a **train** and then a **subway** to visit Grandpa in a small **village**. The trip was very **far**, nearly three hours. Grandpa\'s house sat **between** two green hills beside a stream. Kai loved to **ride** his bicycle there and **exercise** in the fresh air every morning.

Grandpa was a retired teacher who used to **teach** music to children. \'Why do you look so worried?\' he asked. \'I have to **prepare** for the performance, but I am not good **enough**,\' Kai sighed. Grandpa smiled. \'The **difference** separating a good player from a great one is heart, not talent. Just **relax** and let the notes flow.\'

That evening, Kai practiced **outside** under the stars. \'Music is a gift to **share**,\' Grandpa said. Kai **decide**d to try his best. He got up **early** every day, and **sometimes** he practiced late into the night. His fingers moved **quickly** across the keys, and slowly his confidence grew.

Before Kai had to **leave**, Grandpa gave him a small wooden metronome. \'I **believe** in you,\' he said. Back in the city, Kai began to **wear** his favorite blue shirt to every practice. He tried to **remember** every note and promised to **make** the music beautiful. The **idea** of failing still scared him, but he knew he was **lucky** to have so much love around him.

The big day finally came. Kai stood backstage, feeling **lonely** even though the hall was crowded. \'What if I fail?\' he whispered. His friend Lily **notice**d his fear. \'You are not alone,\' she said. \'Just **imagine** you are playing for Grandpa. Your hard work will **matter** more than anything else.\'

Lily\'s words **remind**ed him of Grandpa\'s kindness. \'Take a **break**,\' she said. \'Sit down and **rest** for a minute. Every great **journey** starts with one brave step. I know you have the **courage** inside you.\' Her warm words helped **encourage** him. \'I will **support** you whatever happens,\' she added.

Kai walked onto the stage. He **raise**d his hands above the keys. The hall grew **quiet**, and he began to play. The music flowed like water, soft at first, then strong and bright. When he sang a little, his **voice** was clear and full of feeling. He poured every bit of **effort** into each note.

The last note faded, and for a moment there was silence. Then the whole hall burst into applause. Kai had **succeed**ed! He **realize**d that being brave was not about never feeling fear --- it was about playing anyway. The **experience** taught him more than any lesson in a book.

After the performance, a school reporter interviewed Kai. \'This is a **wonderful** moment,\' he said. \'I was **almost** too scared to walk on stage, but my friends and family gave me strength.\' He hoped that in the **future** he would be a **volunteer** who gives music lessons to children. \'I want to **save** the music in every child\'s heart,\' he added.

That weekend, Kai **return**ed to Grandpa\'s house to thank him. Together they played the old keyboard by the stream. Grandpa was **patient** as always, helping Kai **express** deeper feelings through music. \'You have **discover**ed something precious,\' Grandpa said. \'Never **search** for happiness in distant places --- it is already in your heart. Music will always **protect** you.\'

That evening, the whole town gathered to **celebrate** Kai\'s success. The happy news **spread** from house to house. Grandpa looked deeply **proud** of his grandson, and Kai knew this was a day he would treasure forever.

**Part 2 中文译文**

凯的家里总是充满音乐。每天放学后，他都会在社区中心弹钢琴，还能跟着快歌和妹妹一起跳舞。每逢周五，他甚至能在国际象棋上赢过最好的朋友。但他最大的梦想，是像爷爷一样成为一名音乐家。

一天早上，林老师宣布：\'下个月我们学校要举办才艺演出，我想让你来表演！\'凯既兴奋又紧张。他英语说得好，也会唱歌，但独自表演却不一样。他害怕在这么多人面前弹错音符。

周末，凯先坐火车，再换乘地铁，去一个小村庄看望爷爷。路途非常遥远，将近三个小时。爷爷的房子坐落在两座青山之间，旁边是一条小溪。凯喜欢在那里骑自行车，每天清晨在清新的空气里锻炼。

爷爷是一位退休教师，以前教孩子们音乐。\'你为什么看起来这么担心？\'他问。\'我得为演出做准备，可我还不够好，\'凯叹了口气。爷爷笑着说：\'优秀演奏者和伟大演奏者的区别在于用心，而不在于天赋。放轻松，让音符自然流淌。\'

那天晚上，凯在户外的星空下练习。\'音乐是一份值得分享的礼物，\'爷爷说。凯决定全力以赴。他每天起得很早，有时练到深夜。他的手指在琴键上快速移动，信心也一点点增长。

凯离开前，爷爷送给他一个木制节拍器。\'我相信你，\'他说。回到城里，凯每次练习都穿上自己最喜欢的蓝衬衫。他努力记住每一个音符，并许诺要让音乐变得优美。失败的念头仍让他害怕，但他知道自己很幸运，身边有这么多爱。

大日子终于到了。凯站在后台，尽管大厅里挤满了人，他却感到孤独。\'要是我失败了呢？\'他低声说。朋友莉莉注意到了他的恐惧。\'你不是一个人，\'她说，\'就想象自己是在为爷爷演奏。你的努力比什么都重要。\'

莉莉的话让他想起了爷爷的善良。\'休息一下，\'她说，\'坐下来歇一会儿。每一段伟大的旅程都始于一个勇敢的脚步。我知道你内心有勇气。\'她温暖的话语鼓励了他。\'无论发生什么，我都会支持你，\'她补充道。

凯走上舞台。他在琴键上方抬起双手。大厅安静下来，他开始演奏。音乐如流水般流淌，起初柔和，随后变得强劲而明亮。当他轻轻唱起来时，声音清澈而充满感情。他把全部的努力都倾注在每一个音符里。

最后一个音符落下，那一刻一片寂静。接着，整个大厅爆发出掌声。凯成功了！他意识到，勇敢不是从不感到害怕，而是尽管害怕仍然演奏。这段经历教给他的，比书本上的任何一课都多。

演出结束后，一位校园记者采访了凯。\'这是一个精彩的时刻，\'他说，\'我几乎害怕得不敢上台，但我的朋友和家人给了我力量。\'他希望将来能成为一名志愿者，给孩子们上音乐课。\'我想拯救每个孩子心中的音乐，\'他补充道。

那个周末，凯回到爷爷家感谢他。他们一起在小溪边弹那架旧键盘。爷爷一如既往地耐心，帮助凯通过音乐表达更深的情感。\'你发现了宝贵的东西，\'爷爷说，\'永远不要在远方寻找幸福------它就在你心里。音乐会一直保护着你。\'

那天晚上，整个小镇的人都聚在一起庆祝凯的成功。好消息一家挨一家地传开了。爷爷为孙子感到无比骄傲，而凯知道，这是他会永远珍藏的一天。
