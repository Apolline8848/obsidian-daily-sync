**A Weekend That Changed Everything**

2026年08月14日 \| 人教新目标（Go for It!）初中英语词汇故事

**一、英文故事**

On a **sunny** **weekend**, my friends and I **leave** the city **early**. We **ride** our bikes along a quiet **road** and **cross** an old **bridge** over a clear **river**. The **weather** is perfect, and we **enjoy** every **minute** of the trip.

After about two **hundred** meters, we **decide** to stop at a small **village**. Our tent is only one **kilometer** from here, near a **mountain** and a deep **forest**. The villagers **offer** us some hot tea and **show** us the way to a beautiful **lake**. We **spend** the whole morning there, and then we **climb** a small hill to see the view.

Near the water, we **practice** our favorite **activity**: playing the **guitar** and singing. I **feel** a little **nervous** because I **choose** to **dance** for the first time, but my friends **encourage** me. It is a **wonderful** **feeling**.

In the evening, the sky becomes **cloudy**, so we rest in our tents. We **notice** a kind **stranger** walking by the shore. Later, some local children **visit** us and bring three **cute** rabbits.

The children sit with us around the fire and tell us an old **story**. We **write** it down in our notebooks. They also share a **secret**: this place is **famous** for its tall, old trees.

The next morning, we **volunteer** to help clean up the shore. We meet an old man who looks **lonely** and **worried** because he cannot find his dog. We **happen** to find the dog behind a big tree. The old man is very **polite** and thanks us again and again. It brings us deep **satisfaction** and **joy**.

We **camp** one more night and **hope** to stay longer, but on Sunday we must **drive** home because there is no **train** to this area. The **journey** back is quiet and peaceful.

Suddenly, we **discover** a big tree blocking the path. We stay **calm** and work **together**. My friend Tom is **brave** and **strong**, so he lifts the tree away. In the end, we **achieve** our goal and get home safely.

The **result** of our trip is a small but real **difference** in our class: now more students want to help others. Our teachers **praise** us and say they are **proud** of us. We are filled with great **pride**, because we understand the true **value** of helping people.

We also learn to be **patient** and **honest** with people, and to pay **attention** to those who need help. This is the most important **knowledge** we bring home from the trip.

**二、中文译文**

在一个晴朗的周末，我和朋友们早早地离开了城市。我们沿着一条安静的小路骑自行车，穿过一座横跨清澈河流的旧桥。天气很好，我们享受着旅途中的每一分钟。

骑了大约两百米后，我们决定在一个小村庄停下来。我们的帐篷离这里只有一公里，附近有一座山和一片茂密的森林。村民们给我们端来热茶，并指给我们去看一个美丽湖泊的路。我们在那里度过了整个上午，然后爬上一座小山去看风景。

在水边，我们练习最喜欢的活动：弹吉他和唱歌。我感到有点紧张，因为我第一次选择跳舞，但朋友们都鼓励我。这是一种美妙的感觉。

到了晚上，天空变得多云，于是我们回到帐篷里休息。我们注意到岸边有一位和善的陌生人。后来，几个当地的孩子来看望我们，还带来三只可爱的兔子。

孩子们和我们一起围坐在篝火旁，给我们讲了一个古老的故事。我们把它记在笔记本上。他们还分享了一个秘密：这个地方以高大古老的树木而闻名。

第二天早上，我们自愿帮忙清理岸边。我们遇到一位老人，他看起来孤独又担心，因为他找不到自己的狗了。我们碰巧在一棵大树后面找到了那只狗。老人非常有礼貌，一遍又一遍地感谢我们。这带给我们深深的满足感和快乐。

我们又露营了一晚，希望能多待几天，但星期天我们必须开车回家，因为这里不通火车。返程的旅途安静而平和。

突然，我们发现一棵大树挡住了小路。我们保持冷静，齐心协力。我的朋友汤姆又勇敢又强壮，他把树搬开了。最后，我们实现了目标，安全到家。

我们这次旅行的结果是班上发生了一个虽小却真实的改变：现在有更多的同学想要帮助别人。老师们表扬了我们，并说为我们感到自豪。我们感到无比骄傲，因为我们懂得了帮助他人的真正价值。

我们还学会了待人要有耐心、要诚实，并去关注那些需要帮助的人。这是我们从这次旅行中带回家最重要的知识。
