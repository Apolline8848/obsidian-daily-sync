**Day 45: The Rebirth of the Southern Village**

英语词汇小故事 · 第45天（70词）

**第一部分　英文故事**

Maya, a kind **educator**, returned to her **southern** and **mountainous** hometown. The **agricultural** village traced its **origin** to an ancient **civilization**, but its **existence** now rested on a few old farms. As a **representative** of a charity, Maya brought a plan of **conservation** and **construction**, and the whole **workforce** gathered to greet her, believing the **survival** of their way of life depended on it.

She spoke with great **accuracy** about the **topic** of rebuilding, and her **oral** report was clear. Her plan followed a **systematic** order and met every **requirement**, **regulation**, and local **restriction**. One point was **controversial**: a new road would cut the forest. Some called it a serious **drawback**, while others said it was the only **valid** choice. After patient **comprehension** and careful **correction**, they agreed on an **addition**: a **variety** of young trees would be planted.

She honoured the village **custom** of meeting at dawn, and she set a clear **criterion** for every **deed** of work. Her **preference** was to keep the **process** simple and honest, for she understood the people\'s **tendency** to distrust strangers. An elder said the **depth** of her kindness would be remembered, and this quiet **statement** raised her **status** among them.

Then a sudden **quake** shook the hills. Stones and **powder** fell, and the ground split into a **pile** of rubble. A heavy **chain** and a length of **cloth** saved two workers from the edge of a **vertical** cliff. The village faced a **puzzle**: how to **transport** food and water across the broken paths? Maya did not **flee**, and she never stayed **passive** or **unhappy**; she worked through the night, refusing every **temptation** to rest.

By morning the **probable** danger had passed, but the **suffering** was clear. One old man lay **unconscious**, and a child felt **uncomfortable** in the cold. Maya wrapped him in a **sheet** and gave him a warm **mask** against the dust. She felt a deep **weakness** in her own legs, yet she was **unable** to stop. She thought of the long **evolution** of her village and the story of **mankind**, and her **visual** memory of home gave her strength.

Weeks later, the village began to rebuild. The **version** of the plan had changed, and even the **contrary** opinions now joined in. The **changeable** weather and the **primitive** tools made the work **tough**, but the people showed their **characteristic** patience. They planted a **gardening** plot beside the new well, and the fresh green leaves were a **specific** sign of hope. Nature, however, had one last **trick**: a late frost. Still, no one wished to **mourn** the past, for the **ending** of the old village was the start of something new, and the children now called Maya their **heroine**.

**第二部分　中文译文**

玛雅是一位和蔼的教育家，回到了她位于南方、群山环抱的家乡。这座以农业为主的村庄，起源可追溯到古老的文明，但如今它的存续仅仅维系在几座老农场之上。作为一家慈善机构的代表，玛雅带来了一份保护与建设的计划，全村的劳动力都聚拢来迎接她，因为他们相信，自己生活方式的存续正系于此。

在谈论重建这个话题时，她讲得极为准确，口头的报告条理清晰。她的计划遵循着系统的步骤，符合每一项要求、规章和当地限制。只有一个地方引起了争议：一条新路将要穿过森林。有人认为这是个严重的弊端，也有人认为这是唯一有效的选择。经过耐心的理解和细心的改正，大家商定了一项补充方案：将种植多种多样的幼树。

她尊重村里黎明集会的习俗，并为每一项工作行为定下了明确的标准。她倾向于让整个过程简单而诚实，因为她理解村民们对外来者心存疑虑的倾向。一位长者说，她善良的深度会被人铭记，而这番平静的话语提升了她在众人心中的地位。

接着，一场突如其来的震动撼动了群山。石块和粉末纷纷落下，地面裂成了一堆瓦砾。一条沉重的链条和一截布救下了两名工人，使他们免于坠下垂直的悬崖。村子面临着一道难题：如何把食物和水运过那些断裂的小路？玛雅没有逃走，也从不消极或沮丧；她彻夜劳作，拒绝了一切想歇息的诱惑。

到了早晨，很可能的危险已经过去，但苦难清晰可见。一位老人昏迷不醒，一个孩子在寒冷中感到很不舒服。玛雅用一张薄毯裹住孩子，又给他戴上暖和的口罩抵挡灰尘。她感到自己双腿深深发软，却无法停下。她想起村庄漫长的演变，想起整个人类的故事，脑海中关于家乡的视觉记忆给了她力量。

几周后，村庄开始了重建。计划的版本已经改变，就连原本持相反意见的人也加入进来。多变的天气和简陋的工具让活儿变得棘手，但人们展现出他们特有的耐心。他们在新水井旁开辟了一块园艺地，嫩绿的新叶便是希望的明确信号。然而大自然还有最后一个把戏：一场晚霜。尽管如此，没有人再愿意哀悼过去，因为旧村庄的结局正是新生活的开端，孩子们如今都称玛雅为他们的女英雄。
