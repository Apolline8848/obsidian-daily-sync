**A Wonderful Summer Camp Adventure**

2026年06月22日 \| 人教新目标初中英语词汇故事

Last **Sunday**, I went to **summer** **camp** for the first time. The **woods** and **mountain** had such **clean** **wind**. We stayed in a wooden **cabin** with **bunk** **beds**. After I **unpacked** my **belongings**, a **counselor** **introduced** the **schedule** of **activities**. This would be a great **adventure**!

We went **hiking** along a forest **trail**. Beside a **stream**, there was a wooden **bridge**. We **crossed** it and **discovered** a beautiful **waterfall**. The water was so **clear** that we could see **fishes** swimming. Some kids **swam** in a small **pond**. We also **observed** **butterflies** and **dragonflies**. We saw **wild** **berries** and **picked** some. How wonderful **nature** is!

The next **challenge** was the **obstacle** **course**. We **crawled** under **ropes**, **climbed** over a **net**, and worked with **teammates**. Our coach **urged** us on. We had to **solve** a **puzzle** and then **built** a **raft** with **oars**. We **rowed** it **across** the **lake**. What a **triumph**!

At night, we sat around the **campfire** and **gathered** in a **circle**. We **roasted** **marshmallows** and **sang** **songs** with an **instrument**. **Storytelling** time came --- someone told **ghost** **tales** that were **frightening**. I tried to be **brave**. Looking up at the **starry** **sky**, I knew these **moments** would stay **forever**.

**中文译文**

上周日，我第一次去了夏令营。树林和山间有着如此清爽的风。我们住在一间带上下铺床的小木屋里。我打开行李放好物品后，一位辅导员介绍了活动日程。这一定是一次很棒的冒险！

我们沿着森林小径徒步旅行。在小溪边有一座木桥。我们穿过桥，发现了一个美丽的瀑布。水如此清澈，我们都能看到鱼在游动。一些孩子在一个小池塘里游泳。我们还观察了蝴蝶和蜻蜓。我们看到了野生的浆果，摘了一些。大自然是多么奇妙啊！

下一个挑战是障碍赛赛道。我们在绳子下爬行，翻过网子，和队友们一起合作。教练催促着我们。我们需要解开一个谜题，然后建造了一艘带桨的木筏。我们划着它穿过了湖面。真是一次胜利！

晚上，我们围坐在篝火旁，聚成一个圆圈。我们烤了棉花糖，用乐器弹唱着歌曲。讲故事的时间到了------有人讲了吓人的鬼故事。我努力保持勇敢。抬头望着布满星星的天空，我知道这些时刻将会永远留存。
