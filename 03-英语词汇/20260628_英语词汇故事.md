**A Wonderful School Art Festival**

2026年06月28日 \| 人教新目标初中英语词汇故事

Last **Friday**, our school\'s **art** **festival** **finally** **arrived**! The **hall** was beautifully **decorated** with **colorful** **balloons** and **posters** **hung** on the **walls**. A big **stage** was set up. Everyone **prepared** for the **performance**. Actors wore special **costumes** and waited **behind** the **curtain**.

First, the school **band** **played** the **guitar** and **drums**. The sweet **melody** **filled** the room. The **audience** **clapped** **loudly**. Then **followed** a **dance** **group** performing a **folk** **traditional** dance. Their **graceful** **movements** and beautiful **costume** **attracted** everyone\'s eyes.

In another **corner**, student **paintings** were **displayed**. Some **drew** a beautiful **landscape**; others **painted** a **portrait** of an **owl** in the **forest**. Their **creative** works showed great skill with a **brush** and **ink**. One student\'s **masterpiece** was so amazing that the **judges** found it **difficult** to **choose** a **winner**.

The **drama** by student **actors** was the highlight. They **performed** a funny **role** **bravely** and **memorized** all their **lines** **perfectly**. The **crowd** **cheered** loudly. Everyone felt **proud** of this great **success**. After the **celebration**, student **volunteers** **cleaned** up. **Everyone** **agreed** it was the best art festival ever!

**中文译文**

上周五，我们学校的艺术节终于到了！礼堂装饰得非常漂亮，五颜六色的气球和海报挂在墙上。一个大舞台已经搭好了。每个人都为表演做好了准备。演员们穿着特别的戏服，在幕布后面等待着。

首先，学校乐队演奏了吉他和鼓。甜美的旋律充满了整个房间。观众们热烈地鼓掌。接下来是一个舞蹈团表演了一段民间传统舞蹈。他们优雅的动作和美丽的服装吸引了所有人的目光。

在另一个角落，展出了学生们的画作。有的人画了美丽的风景画；还有的人画了森林中猫头鹰的肖像。他们充满创意的作品展现了运用画笔和墨水的娴熟技巧。一个学生的杰作非常出色，评委们都觉得很难选出获胜者。

学生演员们表演的话剧是整场的高潮。他们勇敢地扮演了一个有趣的角色，完美地记住了所有台词。观众们大声欢呼。每个人都为这巨大的成功感到自豪。庆祝活动结束后，学生志愿者们打扫了场地。大家都一致认为这是有史以来最好的艺术节！
