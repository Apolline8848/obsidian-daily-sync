**The Journey to Green Village**

**一、英文故事**

Last Saturday morning, the **weather** was **cloudy**, but by noon the sky became **sunny** and **windy**. I woke up **early** and **decided** to **spend** the weekend doing something meaningful, so I joined our school\'s **volunteer** team. Our teacher told us a small **secret**: we would **visit** a **famous** museum and then **camp** in a mountain **village**.

At eight o\'clock, my classmates and I met at the **station**. We **left** the city by **train**, and a **hundred** kilometers later we reached the mountains. To reach the small houses, we had to **cross** an old wooden **bridge** over a wide **river**. The bridge shook, and I **felt** **nervous**, but Lily held my hand and said, \"Be **brave**!\"

Inside the **museum**, we worked as guides. An old **stranger** looked **worried** because he had lost a small bag that held something of great **value**. I paid close **attention** to his words and stayed **patient**. We searched **carefully** and at last **discovered** the bag under a chair. The man smiled with a warm **expression** and said we were kind and **honest**. He **praised** us and thanked us for being so **polite**.

In the afternoon, we met an old woman who lived alone and was **lonely**. We **offered** to carry her heavy water and did the work **together**. Lily said, \"A **strong** **spirit** can move mountains,\" and her words **encouraged** me. At night, the old woman sent us a sweet **message** to say thank you. We **hoped** our small help could make a **wonderful** **difference**, and this was how we could **achieve** real happiness.

On Sunday morning, we **prepared** our bags and **chose** to **climb** the highest hill. We **rode** our bikes **along** a small path, and at every **turn** we stopped to look at the view. Every **minute** was filled with laughter. In the evening, our music **club** put on a little **show**: Lily played the **guitar**, some friends **danced**, and I tried to **swim** in the cool water. We had **practiced** for a week and asked everyone to **write** one wish on a card. We all **enjoyed** the night.

On Sunday evening, we **drove** back **across** the green fields toward home. I understood something **true**: kindness always brings a good **result**, and great things **happen** when we share. My parents and teachers were **proud** of us. The deep **satisfaction** and **joy** in my heart came not from prizes but from using our **knowledge** and warm hearts to help others.

**二、中文译文**

上周六早上，天气多云，但到了中午，天空变得晴朗且有风。我早早醒来，决定把周末花在做有意义的事情上，于是加入了学校的志愿者团队。老师告诉了我们一个小秘密：我们将参观一座著名的博物馆，然后在一个山村露营。

八点钟，我和同学们在车站集合。我们乘火车离开城市，一百公里后到达了山区。为了到达那些小屋，我们不得不穿过一座横跨宽宽河流的旧木桥。桥晃动着，我感到紧张，但莉莉握着我的手说：勇敢点！

在博物馆里，我们担任向导。一位年迈的陌生人看起来很担心，因为他丢了一个装有贵重物品的小包。我认真倾听他的话，并保持耐心。我们仔细搜寻，最后在椅子下发现了那个包。老人露出了温暖的表情，说我们善良而诚实。他表扬了我们，并感谢我们如此有礼貌。

下午，我们遇到了一位独自生活、感到孤独的老奶奶。我们主动提出帮她提沉重的水，并一起完成了这项工作。莉莉说：坚强的精神能移山。她的话鼓励了我。晚上，老奶奶给我们发来一条温馨的信息表示感谢。我们希望自己小小的帮助能带来美好的不同，这就是我们实现真正幸福的方式。

星期天早上，我们准备好行囊，选择去爬最高的山丘。我们沿着一条小路骑自行车，每到一处拐弯就停下来看看风景。每一分钟都充满欢声笑语。晚上，我们的音乐社团举行了一场小演出：莉莉弹吉他，一些朋友跳舞，我则试着在清凉的水里游泳。我们练习了一周，还请大家把愿望写在一张卡片上。我们都尽情享受了这个夜晚。

星期天晚上，我们开车穿过绿色的田野回家。我明白了一个真实的道理：善良总会带来好的结果，而当我们分享时，美好的事情就会发生。我的父母和老师都为我们感到自豪。我心中深深的满足与喜悦，并非来自奖品，而是来自用我们的知识和温暖的心去帮助他人。
