**A Summer That Changed Everything**

Last summer, our school **club** organized a trip to a mountain **village**. The **journey** began with an **early** **train** **ride** through the countryside. When we arrived, the **weather** was perfect --- **sunny** yet gently **windy**. Our camp lay **between** a clear **river** and a deep green forest. We had to **cross** a stone **bridge** to enter, where a colorful **sign** welcomed us warmly.

I came as a **volunteer** and felt quite **nervous** at first. Our leader, a **famous** musician, could play the **guitar** and **drum** beautifully. Each day, we **spent** a **hundred** **minutes** doing sports. We learned to **swim**, **climb** rocks, and **dance** to cheerful music. From the hill **top**, we could **enjoy** a **wonderful** view of the entire valley. One morning, I walked **across** a green field and **noticed** a shy boy sitting alone by a tree.

The boy named Ming looked **lonely**, his **expression** full of quiet sadness. I **decided** to approach him with a **polite** greeting. \"Would you like to play **chess**?\" I asked gently. We held a **competition** --- each team had to **write** a short **story**. Our team worked **together**, and Ming contributed a wonderfully **creative** idea. I **encouraged** him to **show** his work to the others. \"You have a real **ability** to **create**,\" I told him. Seeing his **active** participation filled me with pure **joy**.

Ming agreed to let me help him **prepare** for the art show. I **offered** to **drive** him to the nearby town to **visit** a professional artist. The artist gave him high **praise** and said he had **enough** skill to **achieve** great things. Ming was so moved that he **shook** my hand, his eyes wet with tears. I felt a deep sense of **satisfaction** --- a **feeling** stronger than any award. Helping him **discover** his own confidence made me feel truly **strong**. On our final day, we sat under a **calm** evening sky and I told him to always be **brave** and **honest** with himself. Before we had to **leave** the village, I promised to **review** his progress and share my **knowledge** whenever he needed. As we drove through the beautiful **country**side, Ming finally **chose** to smile --- and I knew everything had changed.

**中文译文**

去年夏天，我们学校的俱乐部组织了一次前往山村的旅行。旅途从清晨的火车之旅开始。到达时天气极好------阳光明媚，微风轻拂。我们的营地位于清澈的河流和郁郁葱葱的森林之间。我们跨过一座石桥进入营地，那里有一块色彩鲜艳的标牌热情地欢迎我们。

我作为志愿者来到这里，起初感到相当紧张。我们的领队是一位著名的音乐家，吉他弹奏和鼓点敲击都非常优美。每天，我们花一百分钟进行体育活动。我们学习游泳、攀岩，并随着欢快的音乐跳舞。从山顶上，我们可以欣赏到整个山谷的壮丽景色。一天早晨，我穿过一片绿草地，注意到一个害羞的男孩独自坐在树下。

这个名叫明的男孩看起来很孤独，脸上满是淡淡的忧伤。我决定用礼貌的问候走近他。\"你想下国际象棋吗？\"我轻声问道。下棋时，我试着与他交谈，能感觉到他隐藏着一个深深的秘密。第二天，我邀请明参加一个小组活动。我们举办了一场比赛------每个小组需要写一个短故事。我们小组齐心协力，明贡献了一个非常有创意的点子。我鼓励他把自己的作品展示给大家看。\"你有真正的创造能力，\"我告诉他。看到他积极参与，我心中充满了纯粹的快乐。

明同意让我帮他准备艺术展。我主动提出开车带他去附近的镇上拜访一位专业艺术家。艺术家给了他很高的赞扬，说他有足够的才华去实现伟大的成就。明感动得握住了我的手，眼中含着泪水。我感受到了一种深深的满足------这种感觉比任何奖项都更强烈。帮助他发现自己的自信让我感到真正坚强。在我们最后一天，我们坐在宁静的夜空下，我告诉他要永远勇敢、诚实地面对自己。在我们不得不离开村子之前，我承诺会回顾他的进步，并在任何时候分享我的知识。当我们开车穿过美丽的乡村时，明终于选择了微笑------我知道一切都变了。
