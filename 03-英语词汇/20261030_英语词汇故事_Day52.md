**Day 52: Around the World with Aunt Lily**

英语词汇小故事 · 第52天（70词）

**第一部分：英文故事**

My aunt Lily is an **ambassadress**, and last week she came home after a long journey. Her plane landed gently on the **apron**, and I ran to the **information desk** to ask about her. A kind worker said she was still talking to someone, so I looked for her near the **telephone booth**, but she was really calling on her **mobile phone**. She smiled and asked, \"Do you know the **zip code** of our town?\" Then she put an old **cassette** into her bag and said, \"Let us go home.\"

The next day I told my class about my aunt\'s travels. \"She has been to **Africa**, **Egypt** and the **Middle East**,\" I said. \"She stood beside the **Egyptian** pyramids, then visited **France** and its capital **Paris**. She loved **Greece** and its **Greek** food, **Italy** and **Italian** songs, **Spain** and **Spanish** dancing, **Britain** and the **British** people, **Ireland** and the **Irish**, and **Scotland** with its **Scottish** music. She also saw **Belgium** and **Switzerland** with their high mountains, then went on to **Russia** as far as **Moscow**, and even reached **Iceland**.\"

\"Then she crossed **the Atlantic Ocean** on a great **tanker** and landed in **New York**. She flew over **Mexico**, where she met a friendly **Mexican** family, and continued across the **Pacific** to **New Zealand** and the islands of **Oceania**. In the far south she nearly reached **Antarctica**, and in the far north she stood by **the Arctic**, close to **the North Pole** and beside **the Arctic Ocean**.\"

At home that weekend we had a cheerful time. My cousins and I played **card games**, **hide-and-seek** and **skipping**, and we went **roller skating** in the yard. My brother, who likes **body-building**, used a **roller** to paint the wall green. In the kitchen, Mother opened a can with a **can opener** and dropped the empty can into the **garbage can**. She cooked some **veal** in a **stainless steel** pot, and she told us the factory nearby turns **raw material** into warm cloth. \"No **alcoholic** drinks for children,\" she said, \"here is a **pint** of milk and some **melon seed** for everyone.\" We all remembered our **table manners** and thanked her.

At dinner, Aunt Lily said, \"In every country there are **left-wing** and **right-wing** parties, and there is always a **possibility** of peace.\" She once saw an official receive a **suspension** for breaking the rules, and she visited a **Catholic** church in Europe. The next morning I walked to school, took a wrong **turning**, went along a narrow **side road**, and found a **sideway** that led me back. At the sports meeting the starting **shot** rang out, and I borrowed a **pencil sharpener** for my pencil. My coat was wet, so the teacher let me use a **drier** in the **fitting room**, and I heard the morning news on a **short wave** radio.

**第二部分：中文译文**

我的姑妈莉莉是一位女大使，上周她结束了漫长的旅程回到家乡。她的飞机缓缓降落在停机坪上，我跑到问讯处询问她的情况。一位和蔼的工作人员说她还在和别人通话，于是我走到电话亭附近找她，但她其实是在用手机打电话。她笑着问我：「你知道我们镇的邮政区号吗？」然后她把一盒旧磁带放进包里，说：「我们回家吧。」

第二天，我把姑妈的旅行讲给全班同学听。我说：「她去过非洲、埃及和中东。她站在埃及的金字塔旁，后来又游览了法国和它的首都巴黎。她喜欢希腊和希腊美食、意大利和意大利歌曲、西班牙和西班牙舞蹈、英国和英国人、爱尔兰和爱尔兰人，以及苏格兰和苏格兰音乐。她还游览了比利时和瑞士，看到了它们高高的群山，然后一路到了俄罗斯，最远到达莫斯科，甚至还到过冰岛。」

「后来她乘一艘巨大的油船横渡大西洋，抵达纽约。她飞越墨西哥，在那里结识了一个友好的墨西哥家庭，又继续横跨太平洋，到达新西兰和大洋洲的各个岛屿。在遥远的南方，她几乎到达南极洲；在遥远的北方，她站在北极附近，靠近北极点，就在北冰洋旁边。」

那个周末我们在家里度过了愉快的时光。我和堂兄弟姐妹们玩了纸牌游戏、捉迷藏和跳绳，还在院子里滑了旱冰。我哥哥喜欢健美，他用一个滚筒把墙刷成了绿色。在厨房里，妈妈用开罐器打开了一个罐头，把空罐子扔进了垃圾桶。她在不锈钢锅里煮了一些小牛肉，还告诉我们附近的工厂把原料加工成暖和的布料。「孩子不能喝含酒精的饮料，」她说，「这是给你们每个人的一品脱牛奶和一些瓜子。」我们都记住了餐桌礼节，并向她道谢。

晚饭时，姑妈说：「每个国家都有左翼和右翼政党，而和平总是有希望的。」她曾见过一位官员因违反规定而被停职，还在欧洲参观过一座天主教堂。第二天早上我步行去上学，在一个拐弯处转错了方向，沿着一条狭窄的人行道走，又找到一条岔路把我带了回去。运动会上发令枪响了，我还借了一个转笔刀削铅笔。我的外套湿了，老师让我在试衣间里用烘干机，我还用一台短波收音机收听了早间新闻。
