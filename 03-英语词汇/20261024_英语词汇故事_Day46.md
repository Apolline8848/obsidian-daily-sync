**Day 46: The Keeper of the Lighthouse**

英语词汇小故事 · 第46天（70词）

**第一部分　英文故事**

After her **graduation**, Lily returned to the **inland** village where her grandfather kept the lighthouse. Soon to retire, he would hand the **section** of coast to others. In his pocket lay a new **license** and a **pension** letter; the **outer** wall stood firm. A wide **range** of flowers covered every **acre** of the sea **defence**, and he gave her a warm **greeting**.

Beside the tower stood a museum about a mighty **empire** that once ruled these waters. Its last **emperor** built the **royal** harbour, and after his **funeral** a gentle **revolution** brought **liberation** from **slavery**. The old **religion** faded, and one **corrupt** minister tried to sell the shore, but the villagers never forgot. An old **bomb** lay behind glass, and Lily loved these **diverse**, **abstract** tales, even when records were **contradictory**; she was proud of the **nuclear** station powering the town.

One winter night the sea began to **attack** the coast. A **horrible** storm gathered; **disturbing** news arrived: a fishing boat was heading for a **collision** with the rocks. The **rate** of wind rose, and a **quantity** of rain fell. The lamp\'s **electrical** system failed to **function**. Every **civilian** felt the **catastrophe** coming; the old keeper, weak in **hearing** and **eyesight**, could hardly read the **scene** below.

Lily seized a **flashlight** and lifted the **receiver** of the radio to make an **enquiry** to the coastguard. A rescue **aircraft** and a **helicopter** entered the **airspace** above the bay, while a light **aluminium** boat raced to the **spot** where the **boating** crew drifted. No one would **blame** the old man for his **refusal** to leave; the boat\'s **separation** from the rocks took minutes. Villagers carried blankets as **protection** against the cold.

Only one fisherman suffered a light **injury**, and a **wounded** gull was found on the sand; a young **hunter** wrapped it in his coat. The old keeper, his fingers **numb** with cold, began to **sob** with relief. He had once held the **rank** of captain, and he did not need to **swear**, for everyone trusted him. The villagers helped him **rent** a new lamp and **download** a map of the broken wire; the old beam had started to **rot** and the **tip** of the tower leaned. A strong **brake** was fitted so the light could turn again.

By morning the sea was calm. The village held a festival, and the first **firework** lit the sky while the bonfire burned to soft **ash**. The market sold fresh **export** of dried fish, and Lily saw the **facial** joy of every family, for the rescue was now **compulsory** school learning; none thought her grandfather **mistaken**. He smiled, saying he would **pardon** the sea, and she could **recognise** a quiet peace in his eyes.

**第二部分　中文译文**

毕业之后，莉莉回到了那座内陆村庄，她的祖父在这里看守着灯塔。祖父很快就要退休，把这段海岸交给别人照管。他的口袋里装着一本新执照和一封养老金信，外面的墙壁依旧坚固。海堤的每一英亩土地上开满了各式各样的野花，他给了孙女一个温暖的问候。

灯塔旁矗立着一座博物馆，讲述着一个曾经统治这片海域的庞大帝国。最后一位皇帝修建了那座皇家海港，在他葬礼之后，一场温和的革命为人民带来了从奴隶制度中的解放。古老的宗教衰落，一位腐败的大臣曾试图卖掉这片海岸，但村民们从未忘记。一枚旧炸弹陈列在玻璃柜后，莉莉很喜欢这些多样而抽象的故事，即便有些记载相互矛盾；她也为那座为小镇供电的核电站感到骄傲。

一个冬夜，大海开始袭击海岸。一场可怕的风暴正在聚集；令人不安的消息传来：一艘渔船正朝着礁石撞去。风速迅速上升，大量的雨水倾泻而下。灯塔的电力系统出了故障，无法运转。每个平民都感到灾难正在逼近；那位老看守人听力衰退、视力模糊，几乎看不清下面的情景。

莉莉抓起手电筒，拿起旧无线电的听筒，向海岸警卫队发出询问。一架救援飞机和一架直升机进入了海湾上方的空域，与此同时，一艘轻巧的铝制小船冲向划船船员们漂流的地方。没有人会责备老人拒绝离开；小船与礁石的分开只用了短短几分钟。村民们把毯子搬下来，作为抵御寒冷的保护。

只有一名渔夫受了轻伤，还有一只受伤的海鸥被发现躺在沙滩上，一个年轻的猎人把它裹进外套里。老看守人手指冻得麻木，如释重负地抽泣起来。他曾拥有上尉的军衔，也无需发誓，因为每个人都信任他。村民们帮他租来一盏新灯，下载了一张损坏线路的地图；那根旧木梁已经开始腐烂，塔顶都倾斜了。他们装上一个结实的刹车，大灯终于又能转动了。

到了早晨，海面恢复了平静。村里举办了一场庆祝活动，第一朵烟花点亮了天空，篝火慢慢烧成了柔软的灰烬。市场上出售着新鲜的出口鱼干，莉莉看到每家每户脸上洋溢着喜悦，因为这次救援已成为学校里的必修课；没有人再认为她的祖父错了。他微笑着说，他要原谅大海，而莉莉能从他眼中辨认出一份宁静的安详。
