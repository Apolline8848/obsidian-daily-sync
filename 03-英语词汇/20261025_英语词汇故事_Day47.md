**Day 47: The Village That Rebuilt Its Library**

英语词汇小故事 · 第47天（70词）

**第一部分　英文故事**

Maya, an **adolescent**, lived in a village that had lost its only library to a fire. The **absence** of books hurt everyone, and for a long **length** of time no one knew what to do. Her grandfather, a retired teacher, became her **coworker**, and together they started an **association** to rebuild it.

They wrote an **agenda** and set one **criterion**: welcome every **category** of reader. The town office called the dream **useless**, but Maya refused. A kind **businessman** gave a large **amount**, and a **federal** **agent** promised a grant. A quiet hope grew into a **nonviolent** **battle** for books.

To raise more, they held a fair. Each **booth** sold stories, and the main **attraction** was a storyteller dressed as a **cowboy**. A **handful** of children sat on a **heap** of cushions and laughed from the **belly**. Maya wrote a **caption** for every photo, and the news was **broadcast** on radio. An **ambassador** from a far land sent a box of books by **airplane**.

Soon the shelves filled with every **alphabet**. Maya loved the **bestseller** about **twin** heroes who crossed a new **dimension** each night. There were **religious** tales, a **technical** corner, and a **theoretical** shelf about space. The club\'s **constitution** promised free **access** to all, and **admission** cost only a smile. Not one **component** was wasted, and every **leftover** plank became a shelf.

The **following** morning, an old woman with a deep **wrinkle** came in a wheelchair; the **disabled** readers\' ramp was the **leading** reason she returned. A **married** couple read aloud, and a young reader felt a sudden **hunger**. Maya wrote the **account** of the year --- a slow **adaptation** of the town --- and made one **adjustment** to the lights. The library became a quiet **amusement** and a strong **bond** between neighbours. A new **trend** spread: every **inch** of wall held a book, and rooms were measured in **cubic** metres.

Some old rules had been **arbitrary**, so the club made them **civil** and fair; nothing was **unfair** now. The **disgusting** smell of smoke faded, and the **unknown** writer became a local hero. One evening a stranger brought a **tentative** plan for a **worldwide** book exchange; Maya welcomed the **alternative**. She showed him each **department**, and he praised the strong **character** of the village. The **carrier** with the parcels stayed for tea and told an **affair** about a lost letter. A boy once said a **vague**, **incorrect** thing about the club, but no one was **unpleasant**; the door stayed open. The old dog did not **bite**, and nobody tried to **abuse** the free books. Because everyone shared, a reader with a **disability** enjoyed the **supreme** joy of stories like anyone.

**第二部分　中文译文**

玛雅是个正值青春期的少女，住在一个村庄里，村里唯一的图书馆在一场火灾中被烧毁了。书籍的缺席让每个人都很伤心，很长一段时间里，谁都不知道该怎么办。她的祖父是一位退休教师，成了她的搭档，他们一起成立了一个协会来重建图书馆。

他们写下一份议程，并定下一条标准：欢迎每一类读者。镇上的办公室说这个梦想毫无用处，但玛雅拒绝放弃。一位善良的商人捐了一大笔钱，一位联邦政府的代表也承诺提供一笔补助。一份安静的希望渐渐变成了一场为书籍而进行的非暴力斗争。

为了筹集更多资金，他们举办了一场集市。每个摊位都出售故事，最吸引人的是一位装扮成牛仔的说书人。几个孩子坐在一堆靠垫上，捧腹大笑。玛雅为每张照片写下说明文字，好消息也在电台播出了。一位来自遥远国度的大使用飞机寄来了一箱书。

很快，书架上摆满了各种文字。玛雅最爱那本关于双胞胎英雄的畅销书，他们每晚都会穿越一个全新的维度。书架上还有宗教故事、一个技术角，以及一个关于太空的理论书架。俱乐部的章程承诺所有人都可以自由进入，而门票只是一个微笑。没有一个部件被浪费，每一块剩余的木板都变成了一层书架。

第二天早晨，一位脸上带着深深皱纹的老妇人坐着轮椅来了；残疾人读者的坡道是她愿意再来的最主要原因。一对已婚夫妇大声朗读，一位小读者突然感到一阵渴望。玛雅写下了这一年的记录------整个小镇慢慢适应的过程------并对灯光做了一处调整。图书馆成了一份安静的娱乐，也成了邻里之间牢固的纽带。一种新潮流传开了：每一英寸墙面都摆上书，房间都用立方米来丈量。

一些旧规矩过去十分武断，于是俱乐部把它们改得文明而公平；如今再没有不公平的事。令人作呕的烟味渐渐散去，那位曾经默默无闻的作家成了当地的英雄。一天傍晚，一位陌生人带来了一个关于全球图书交换的初步计划；玛雅很欢迎这个备选方案。她带他参观了每一个部门，他称赞了这座村庄的优良品格。那位送来包裹的送货员留下来喝茶，讲了一桩关于一封丢失信件的怪事。有个男孩曾经说了一句含糊又不正确的话，但没有人生气；大门始终敞开。那条老狗从不咬人，也没有人想过滥用这些免费的书。因为人人都乐于分享，一位身有残疾的读者也能像其他人一样，享受到故事带来的至高喜悦。
