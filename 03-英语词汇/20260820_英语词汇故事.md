**The Night We Found Leo**

2026年08月20日 \| 人教新目标（Go for It!）初中英语词汇故事

**一、英文故事**

Last Friday, the **weather** report said it would be **sunny**, but when we woke up, the sky was already **cloudy** and **windy**. We still **decide** to go camping in the **mountain**, because we had prepared for weeks.

We **leave** home very **early** and **drive** to the foot of the hill. The **road** is **far** but **pretty**, and we sing songs **together** all the way.

When we arrive, the ranger points to a good spot. \'I **suggest** you **camp** near the **lake**,\' he says. It lies **between** two green hills, **north** of a small **village**. The **forest** smells fresh and the **river** sings over smooth stones.

After lunch we **climb** a small hill and **cross** a narrow **bridge** over a stream. Tom wants to **ride** his bike, but we tell him to **walk** slowly and be **careful** on the muddy path. We **spend** the whole afternoon hiking **along** the valley and **across** a green field, watching birds and clouds.

Suddenly we **notice** that our friend Leo is missing. At first we think it is no big **problem**, but one **minute** turns into ten, and ten into a **hundred**. Leo did not bring **enough** water or a warm coat. A cold **feeling** grows in our hearts, and we know he must be **lonely** and **hungry** out there alone. The hills can be **terrible** at night, so we must find him quickly.

Everyone wants to **run** and **search** everywhere, but our teacher says we must stay **calm**. She tells us the most **important** rule of a **journey**: never go alone. We split into groups and make a plan.

A little way ahead, we find a **sign** pointing **south** to a deep valley. Then we hear a weak **voice** calling from below. We send Leo a **message** on our phones, but there is no signal. We wonder what will **happen** to him, and we **hope** he can hear our shouts.

It takes all our **courage** to go down the steep rocks, but we **encourage** each other with kind words. We find Leo sitting by a small stream. He is **brave** and **strong**, but his ankle is **hurt**, so two of us carry him up the slope.

We **return** to the campsite as the stars come out. A **volunteer** nurse in our group cleans Leo\'s ankle and says he needs to rest. Leo smiles and says he never felt in real **trouble**, because he knew we would **protect** him.

That night around the fire, we **realize** the true **value** of friendship. We feel great **joy** and a quiet **pride** in what we did, and the starry sky looks **wonderful** above us. Everyone gave their best **effort**, and we **support** each other like a real team.

From this trip we **discover** that we can **create** our own safety and happiness by working as one. We also learn useful **knowledge** about the wild, and we **try** to write it all in our notebooks so we will not forget.

**二、中文译文**

上周五，天气预报说会是晴天，但当我们醒来时，天空已经多云而且刮风了。我们仍然决定去山里露营，因为我们已经准备了好几个星期。

我们很早就离开家，开车前往山脚下。这条路很远但很漂亮，我们一路一起唱歌。

到达后，管理员给我们指了一个好位置。\'我建议你们在湖边露营，\'他说。这片湖坐落在两座绿色的山丘之间，在一个小村庄的北边。森林里的空气清新，河水在光滑的石头上歌唱。

午饭后，我们爬上一座小山，跨过一条狭窄的小桥。汤姆想骑他的自行车，但我们让他在泥泞的小路上慢慢走，要小心。我们整个下午沿着山谷徒步，穿过一片绿色的田野，看鸟儿和云彩。

突然，我们注意到朋友利奥不见了。起初我们觉得这不是什么大问题，但一分钟变成了十分钟，十分钟又变成了一百分钟。利奥没有带足够的水和保暖外套。一股寒意涌上心头，我们知道他一定在外面又孤独又饥饿。山里的夜晚可能很可怕，所以我们必须尽快找到他。

大家都想跑去到处搜寻，但老师说我们必须保持镇定。她告诉我们旅途中最重要的一条规则：绝不能单独行动。我们分成小组，制定了一个计划。

在离这里不远的地方，我们发现了一块指向南边深谷的路牌。然后我们听到从下面传来微弱的呼喊声。我们用手机给利奥发了消息，但没有信号。我们不知道他会怎么样，只希望他能听见我们的喊声。

走下陡峭的岩石需要极大的勇气，但我们用温暖的话语互相鼓励。我们发现利奥坐在一条小溪边。他既勇敢又坚强，但脚踝受伤了，于是我们中的两个人把他背上了山坡。

当星星出来时，我们回到了营地。我们小组里的一位志愿护士帮利奥清理了脚踝，并说他需要休息。利奥笑着说，他从未感到真正的麻烦，因为他知道我们会保护他。

那天晚上，我们在篝火旁意识到友谊的真正价值。我们感到无比的快乐，也为我们所做的事感到自豪，头顶的星空显得格外美丽。每个人都尽了最大的努力，我们像一支真正的队伍一样互相支持。

从这次旅行中，我们发现，只要齐心协力，我们就能创造属于自己的安全和幸福。我们还学到了很多关于野外生存的有用知识，并且努力把它们都写进笔记本里，这样我们就不会忘记了。
