**Day 51: A Year to Remember**

英语词汇小故事 · 第51天（70词）

**第一部分：英文故事**

Last year I became a **school-leaver**, and my town gave me a year to remember. On **Christmas Eve**, our family held a warm **get-together**. We decorated the **Christmas tree**, and I wrote a **Christmas card** for my **step-mother**, a kind **businesswoman**. My **sister-in-law**, who is **chairwoman** of the community club, arrived with a **hand truck** loaded with gifts.

In spring, near **Easter**, we had a reunion at our old **primary school**. The **dining room** was full, and children played on the **see-saw** in the **playroom**. Later I went to the **department store**, where a friendly **saleswoman** helped me choose a **washing machine**, a **tape recorder** and a **walkman**. A **shop assistant** wrapped them carefully.

On the way home I crossed the **ring road**, passed the **roundabout**, and stopped at the **zebra crossing** when the **traffic lights** turned red. At home, my father was not **vain** about his hair; he simply used his **shaver** and a **sharpener** for his pencil. We sorted the **junk mail** and threw away the **junk food**, then opened a cold drink with an **opener**.

In summer we swam in the **swimming pool**, played **ping-pong** in the yard and **table tennis** at the club, where my cousin won a **gold medal**. By **mid-autumn**, the **maple leaves** turned red in the **northeast** and **southwest**. My brother, a quiet **human being**, played a **computer game** on his **microcomputer** and read about the **Arctic** and the **Antarctic**.

At school I studied **French** and **Arabic**. In history we read about an **African** **fighter**, a **westerner**, a **Frenchman**, a **Buddhist** and a **Christian**. Our teacher, a **spokesman** for peace, taught us about **socialism**; he said a true **socialist** thinks of others, and between money and kindness he always chooses the **latter**. He also reminded us that no human being is **cold-blooded**.

In **March**, my uncle took the **shuttle** to the **northwest** to open a **bank account**. The bank charged a small **service charge**, and he waited in the **waiting room** on a sofa of **medium** size. He bought **secondhand** **bedclothes** and warm **beddings** for his new flat. Though he heard news of **unrest** and read about **Buddhism**, he stayed calm and even applied for a **patent** for his small invention.

**第二部分：中文译文**

去年，我成了一名中学毕业生，我的小镇给了我难忘的一年。在圣诞节前夜，我们家举行了一场温馨的聚会。我们装饰了圣诞树，我还为我的继母------一位和蔼的女企业家------写了一张圣诞卡。我的嫂子是社区俱乐部的主席，她带着一辆装满礼物的手推车来了。

春天，临近复活节时，我们在母校小学举行了一次聚会。食堂里坐满了人，孩子们在游戏室里玩跷跷板。后来我去了百货商场，一位友好的女售货员帮我挑选了洗衣机、录音机和随身听。一位售货员把它们仔细地包好。

回家的路上，我穿过环形公路，经过转盘路，在红绿灯变红时停在了斑马线上。在家里，我父亲对自己的头发并不自负；他只是用他的剃须刀和一个卷笔刀削铅笔。我们整理好垃圾邮件，扔掉了垃圾食品，然后用开瓶器打开了一杯冷饮。

夏天，我们在游泳池里游泳，在院子里打乒乓球，在俱乐部里练习乒乓球，我的表姐在那里赢得了一枚金牌。到了中秋，东北和西南的枫叶变红了。我的哥哥是个安静的人，他在微机上玩电子游戏，还阅读关于北极和南极的书。

在学校里，我学习法语和阿拉伯语。历史课上，我们读到一位非洲战士、一位西方人、一位法国人、一位佛教徒和一位基督徒。我们的老师是一位和平发言人，他教我们社会主义；他说，一个真正的社会主义者会为他人着想，在金钱与善良之间，他总是选择后者。他还提醒我们，没有人是冷血的。

三月，我的叔叔乘班车去西北开了一个银行账户。银行收取了一小笔服务费，他在候诊室里一张中等大小的沙发上等候。他为自己的新公寓买了二手的被褥和暖和的卧具。尽管他听到了骚乱的消息，还读了关于佛教的书，但他依然保持平静，甚至为他的小发明申请了一项专利。
