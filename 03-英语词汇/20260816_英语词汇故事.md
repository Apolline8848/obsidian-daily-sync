**一、英文故事**

**The Kindest Journey**

Last **weekend**, our school organized a **wonderful** outdoor **activity** --- a **volunteer** trip. We would **camp** by a **lake** and help clean a small **village**.

I was so excited that I could not **feel** tired. I **left** home **early** and **rode** my bicycle to the **station**. **Together**, we took a **train** out of the city.

The **weather** was **sunny** but a little **windy**. We walked **along** a narrow **road**, and our bus **drove** **across** a **famous** old **bridge**. A clear **river** ran **between** two green hills.

At last, we **crossed** a wide field, **turned** at a **corner**, and began to **climb** a **mountain** path. The **forest** was full of singing birds. It was like a **true** dream, and the view was so **pretty**.

At noon, we were **hungry**, so we **spent** some time eating lunch near the **beach**. Then I **noticed** a **lonely** little dog. It was thin and had no **owner**.

My friend **decided** to help it. We **offered** the dog some food and **prepared** a warm place for it. The little **animal** was so **cute** that we all wanted to keep it. I was **nervous** at first, but I **tried** to be **brave** and **patient**.

Soon an old **stranger** came to us. He told us the dog had been lost for days. We found it, so he thanked us and we all **laughed** with relief. He said our kindness brought him great **joy**.

The old man **admired** our **spirit** and **praised** us for being **honest** and kind. He said that in his life, helping others was the greatest **value**. His words **encouraged** us a lot.

On the way home, I **discovered** a **secret**: helping others brings a warm **feeling** inside. I **hope** to become a person with a **strong** and kind heart, and I learned to **treat** every living thing with care.

We **enjoyed** the whole **journey**, and we **achieved** something important that day. I had **enough** happy memories to last a lifetime. I will always remember what **happened** that day. When I **visited** it again in my dreams, my heart was full of **pride**.

**二、中文译文**

**最温暖的旅程**

上周末，我们学校组织了一次精彩的户外活动------一次志愿者之旅。我们会在湖边露营，并帮助打扫一个小村庄。

我兴奋得一点也不觉得累。我很早就离开了家，骑着自行车去车站。我们一起坐火车出了城。

天气晴朗，但有点风。我们沿着一条狭窄的道路行走，我们的巴士驶过一座著名的旧桥。一条清澈的河流在两座绿色的山丘之间流淌。

最后，我们穿过一片宽阔的田野，在一个拐角处转弯，开始攀登一条山路。森林里满是唱歌的鸟儿。这就像一个真实的梦，景色非常美丽。

中午，我们饿了，于是在海滩附近花了一些时间吃午饭。然后我注意到一条孤独的小狗。它很瘦，没有主人。

我的朋友决定帮助它。我们给了小狗一些食物，为它准备了一个温暖的地方。这只小动物太可爱了，我们都想留下它。一开始我很紧张，但我努力保持勇敢和耐心。

不久，一位年迈的陌生人向我们走来。他告诉我们，这条狗已经走丢好几天了。我们找到了它，所以他感谢了我们，我们都如释重负地笑了。他说，我们的善良给他带来了巨大的喜悦。

这位老人钦佩我们的精神，并表扬我们诚实而善良。他说，在他的一生中，帮助他人是最有价值的事。他的话极大地鼓舞了我们。

在回家的路上，我发现了一个秘密：帮助他人会在内心带来一种温暖的感觉。我希望成为一个拥有坚强而善良心灵的人，我也学会了善待每一个生命。

我们享受了整个旅程，并且在那一天取得了重要的收获。我拥有了足够多的快乐回忆，可以珍藏一生。我会永远记得那天发生的事情。当我在梦中再次造访它时，我的心中充满了自豪。
