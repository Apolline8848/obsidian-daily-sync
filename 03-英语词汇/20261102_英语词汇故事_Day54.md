**Day 54: The Morning of the Marathon**

英语词汇小故事 · 第54天（70词）

**第一部分　英文故事**

**Hello**! **Everyone** in our school woke before **five** on race day. The playground was **soft** after the night rain, and the whole town was **silent** and still. **Dr** Li, our teacher, waved a **bent** old flag, and **everything** was ready for the big run. A bright **ad** on the wall promised a **forty**-**kilometre** course for the **eleven** bravest students. **Oh**, what a long road that was!

At the start line, **few** runners looked calm. The starter counted down: \"**three**, **two**, **one**!\" Then a loud **bang** rang out, and a **thousand** voices cheered while a **hundred** flags flew. **Hooray**! \"**Hey**,\" the crowd shouted, and the runners dashed forward.

Number **six** took the lead, and the **first** runner passed the lake. Soon the **third**, **fourth**, and **fifth** places began to change. The **sixth** and **seventh** runners stayed close, while the **eighth** and **ninth** kept a steady pace. The **twelfth** boy stumbled, and a girl in **sixteenth** place helped him rise. Near the end, the **twentieth** runner crossed the line, and the **twenty-first** followed only **fifteen** seconds later.

The winner\'s time was **twenty-one** minutes and **thirty** seconds. **Seventeen** students finished in under an hour, and **eighteen** more came in before noon. The youngest runner was **thirteen** years old, and the oldest was **fifty**. **Four** teachers ran beside us, and **fourteen** parents rode bicycles along the road. **Nineteen** medals were given, but only **sixty** people stayed for the party, though **eighty** had promised to come and **ninety** more sent a text to say **cheers**.

After the race, **several** students gathered around the **TV** to watch the replay on a **DVD**, a **VCD**, and an old **CD ROM**. Runner **No.** **twenty** said the morning had been **minus** **sixteen** degrees, **plus** a cold wind that **struck** our faces. **Ouch**, my legs hurt! I **like** running, and I told my friends the tale of the first marathon in **BC** days, when only a few **mm** of rain fell on the brave runner. **Ha**, we laughed. A **million** stars and a **billion** dreams cannot measure our joy. Then I said **hi** to a friend, and at last we said **good-bye** and **bye**.

**第二部分　中文译文**

你好！比赛那天，我们学校的每个人都五点前就起床了。夜里下过雨，操场很柔软，整座小镇都寂静无声。李博士老师挥着一面弯曲的旧旗子，一切都为这场长跑做好了准备。墙上一则醒目的广告承诺，要为最勇敢的十一名学生举办一场四十公里的比赛。哦，那该是多么漫长的一段路啊！

起跑线上，没几个选手显得镇定。发令员倒计时：「三、二、一！」接着一声巨响「砰」，一千个声音欢呼起来，一百面旗帜迎风飘扬。好哇！「嘿，」人群呐喊着，选手们向前冲去。

六号选手率先领先，第一个跑过湖边。很快，第三、第四、第五名的位置开始不断变换。第六名和第七名选手紧追不舍，第八名和第九名则保持着稳定的节奏。第十二名男孩绊了一跤，第十六名的那位女孩把他扶了起来。接近终点时，第二十名选手冲过了终点线，第二十一名仅仅十五秒后也到了。

冠军的成绩是二十一分三十秒。十七名学生在不到一小时内完赛，另有十八人在中午前到达。最年轻的选手十三岁，最年长的五十岁。四位老师陪着我们一起跑，十四位家长骑着自行车沿路跟随。颁发了十九枚奖牌，但只有六十人留下来参加聚会，尽管八十人曾答应会来，还有九十多人发短信说「干杯」。

比赛结束后，几个学生围在电视机前，通过一张DVD、一张VCD和一张旧光盘观看回放。二十号选手说，那天清晨是零下十六度，还有一阵冷风刮在脸上。哎哟，我的腿好疼！我喜欢跑步，我给朋友们讲起公元前时代第一场马拉松的故事，那时勇敢的跑者身上只落了几毫米的雨。哈，我们都笑了。一百万个星星、十亿个梦想，也衡量不了我们的快乐。后来我和一位朋友打了声招呼，最后我们互道「再见」，挥手告别。
