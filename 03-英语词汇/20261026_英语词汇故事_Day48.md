**Day 48: The Reopening of Willow Hall**

英语词汇小故事 · 第48天（70词）

**第一部分　英文故事**

Lena returned to Willowbrook to reopen Willow Hall, a building her late grandfather had treasured. The **administration** was slow and **bureaucratic**, the **finance** thin, and the **paperwork** had gathered dust for a **fortnight**. She refused to surrender.

She filled a long **questionnaire**, checked every name in the town **directory**, and sent a **fax** to the **ministry**. A **reviewer** praised the plan, a **typist** typed it up, and a **maid** scrubbed the **washroom** clean. The **founding** of the hall was a proud story, and the new **extension** would hold a small museum for **tourism**. A **translator** wrote the signs, and every **user** could read them. On a dusty shelf sat a box labelled with an old **postcode**.

Money was tight, so she travelled by **rail** to save **petrol**. By **midday** of every **workday** the hall rang with hammers. A former **prisoner** came to help; he had left **violent** days behind and now offered **unconditional** kindness. A **lecture** on local history drew a crowd, and a neighbour donated **jewelry** for the museum. A dark **stain** faded under fresh paint, and the **storage** room filled with treasures; the **surplus** books went to the school. Nearby lay the quiet **burial** ground of the first settlers.

The hall\'s **ownership** had passed to the town, and every **possession** in the museum told a story. After one more **revision** of the plans, Lena gave her **presentation** to the council on the town\'s **behalf**. The only **incident** was a spilled jar of **liquid** that left the floor sticky. The **identification** of the old photographs took a week, and a novel about a **murder** found a shelf by the door. From a high window you could **spy** the whole square, and no one could **divorce** the hall from its happy past.

Still, a small **disagreement** arose over the colours, and the first **mixture** of paint looked **unhealthy**. An **untrue** rumour said the roof was **unsafe**, but Lena found it not **unfit** at all. She listened with **undivided** attention and never made a **random** choice. One **troublesome** afternoon grew **unbearable** in the heat, the thermometer read thirty degrees **centigrade**, but a quiet **prayer** brought her a **spiritual** calm; some called it merely **subjective**, yet everyone felt it. A **cordless** phone rang: it was the **premier**\'s office, confirming the visit. Her **unmarried** aunt, face **sunburnt** from the garden, wiped her brow with a **tissue** and smiled. At the tiny **café**, they raised a **bar** of chocolate, with a **minimum** of fuss and not a single **smelly** corner left. Her first meeting had been **unsuccessful**, and that morning\'s worries felt **unimportant**; still **unwilling** to quit, she found even her **worn** shoes light.

**第二部分　中文译文**

莉娜回到威洛布鲁克，重新开放柳树会堂------那栋她已故祖父生前珍爱的建筑。行政部门办事缓慢又官僚，资金紧张，文书工作积压了两个星期。她不肯放弃。

她填了一份长长的问卷，核对镇名册里的每一个名字，又给政府部门发了一份传真。一位评论者称赞这个计划，一位打字员把方案打了出来，一位女仆把盥洗室擦得干干净净。会堂的创办是一段令人骄傲的往事，而新建的扩建部分将用作一座小博物馆来发展旅游业。一位翻译者写好了指示牌，每一位使用者都能看懂。落满灰尘的书架上放着一个贴着旧邮政编码的盒子。

资金紧张，于是她乘火车出行以节省汽油。每个工作日的正午时分，会堂里都回响着锤声。一位前囚犯前来帮忙；他已经把暴力的日子抛在身后，如今付出无条件的善意。一场关于当地历史的演讲吸引了许多人，一位邻居为博物馆捐来了珠宝。一块深色的污渍在新漆下渐渐褪去，储藏室堆满了宝贝，多出来的书送到了学校。附近就是第一批定居者安静的墓地。

会堂的所有权已经移交给了镇上，博物馆里的每一件财物都在讲述一个故事。在对方案再做一次修改之后，莉娜代表全镇向议会做了演示汇报。唯一的插曲是一只罐子打翻、黏稠的液体洒了一地。老照片的鉴定花了一周时间，一本讲谋杀案的小说被放在了门边的书架上。从一扇高高的窗子望出去，可以窥见整个广场，谁也无法把会堂同它幸福的往事分离开来。

不过，在颜色上还是起了小小的分歧，第一批油漆混合物看起来不太健康。一则不真实的谣言说屋顶不安全，但莉娜发现它根本没有不结实的地方。她全神贯注地倾听，从不做随意的决定。一个恼人的下午热得令人难以忍受，温度计显示三十摄氏度，但一次安静的祈祷带给她一份心灵的宁静；有人说这只是一种主观的感受，可每个人都感觉到了。一部无线电话响了，是首相办公室打来的，确认了来访安排。她那未婚的姑妈脸被花园里的太阳晒得黑黑的，用纸巾擦了擦额头，笑了。在那家小小的咖啡馆里，他们举起一块巧克力来庆祝，没有一点麻烦，也没有留下一个发臭的角落。她的第一次会议并不成功，那天早上的担忧也显得无足轻重了；她始终不愿意放弃，如今连那双穿旧了的鞋子都显得轻快了。
