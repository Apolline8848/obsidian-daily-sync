**Day 49: The Professor\'s Map**

英语词汇小故事 · 第49天（70词）

**第一部分　英文故事**

Mia\'s grandfather had left her a wooden **base** in the garden, a shed packed with **junk**. Behind it stood a rusty **mailbox**, and inside lay a yellow **airmail** letter. A **learned** professor had written to ask for her help with a lost **dynasty** and a hidden treasure.

The letter told of an ancient **battleground** where a **savage** king had spread **violence** and **aggression**. His guards swung a **sword** and hurled a sharp **spear**. A heavy **tank** crawled forward while a **missile** gleamed on the hill. The people faced **starvation** until they rose in a brave **movement** to free their **motherland**.

In that old story, a **thief** stole the golden cup, a **slave** fled through the dark, and a grey **wolf** howled. A **locust** cloud ate the crops while a **cock** crowed at dawn. By a stone **tomb** a **bishop** prayed, and a **fool** laughed for no reason.

Mia copied the map with a blue **crayon** and slipped it into a **file**. She counted every **penny** and filled a **gallon** jug with water. A **businessman** in a **jeep** gave her a lift, and an **operator** put through her call home. At the market she bought a fresh **squid** and a **cigar** for the gatekeeper; the **left-handed** barber gave her a **haircut** for one coin. Her cat slept on her **lap**, and she bit a **fingernail** as she read about the king\'s **vice** and his **rigid** laws. She wiped the dusty page with a clean **rag**.

The professor had written that **mathematics** and **psychology** both help, if one keeps a calm **judgement**. Mia saw that the real prize was the **acquisition** of wisdom. She read of **immigration** and of people woven like **fibre** into one cloth, every **organ** of the tale warm with **flesh** and blood.

That night she dreamed a **fantasy**: a **bat** crossed a **beam** of moonlight, a **birdcage** swung by the door, and a bear stirred from **hibernation**. A **stout**, **overweight** guard dropped his **broken** spear, and a **lame** dog limped past a **man-made** bridge. A **radioactive** rock glowed deep in the ground, and the castle began to **sink**.

She woke with a start, and the professor\'s **means** were finally clear. Where there is **friction** and **racial** hatred, peace turns **tasteless** and **acid**. But a kind gift, even a **tax-free** one, can heal a heart. A **viewer** at the window watched a **part-time** gardener hum as he worked, and she knew that even a **bound** heart can learn to fly.

**第二部分　中文译文**

米娅的祖父在花园里给她留下一块木头根据地，一个堆满破烂的小棚。棚子后面立着一个生锈的邮箱，里面躺着一封黄色的航空邮件。一位博学的教授写信来，请她帮忙寻找一个失落的王朝和一处隐藏的宝藏。

信里讲述了一片古老的战场，一位野蛮的国王在这里散布暴力与侵略。他的卫兵挥舞着剑，掷出锋利的矛。一辆沉重的坦克向前爬行，一枚导弹在山丘上闪闪发光。人们饱受饥饿，直到他们奋起发动一场勇敢的运动，去解放他们的祖国。

在那个古老的故事里，一个小偷偷走了金杯，一个奴隶逃进黑暗，一只灰狼嚎叫。一片蝗虫云吃光了庄稼，一只公鸡在黎明啼叫。在一座石墓旁，一位主教祈祷，一个傻子无缘无故地大笑。

米娅用一支蓝色蜡笔抄下地图，把它塞进一个档案夹。她数了数每一枚便士，又往壶里灌了一加仑水。一位开着吉普车的商人捎了她一程，一位接线员帮她接通了回家的电话。在集市上，她买了一只新鲜的鱿鱼，又给看门人买了一支雪茄；惯用左手的理发师收一枚硬币给她理了发。她的猫睡在她的膝上，她一边读着国王的罪恶和他死板的法律，一边咬着自己的指甲。她用一块干净的抹布擦了擦落满灰尘的信纸。

教授写道，数学和心理学都能帮上忙，只要你能保持冷静的判断。米娅明白，真正的奖赏是获得智慧。她读到移民，读到人们像纤维一样被织进同一块布，故事的每一个器官都带着血肉，充满温度。

那天夜里她做了一个幻想般的梦：一只蝙蝠掠过一束月光，一个鸟笼在门边摇晃，一只熊从冬眠中醒来。一个肥壮又超重的卫兵丢下了他那支折断的矛，一条跛腿的狗一瘸一拐地走过一座人造的桥。一块放射性的岩石在地底深处发光，城堡开始下沉。

她猛地醒来，教授的方法终于清晰了。哪里有摩擦和种族的仇恨，和平就会变得无味而酸涩。但是一份善意的礼物，哪怕是一份免税的礼物，也能治愈一颗心。一个窗边的观看者看着一位兼职园丁哼着歌干活，而她明白，哪怕是一颗被束缚的心，也能学会飞翔。
