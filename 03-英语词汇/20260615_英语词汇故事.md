**A Wonderful School Food Festival**

2026年06月15日 \| 人教新目标初中英语词汇故事

Last **Thursday**, the **lovely** **students** had a **cooking** **contest**. Everyone was **cheerful** on that **morning**. The **classroom** **turned** into a **busy** **kitchen**. **Somebody** **brought** fresh **ingredients** --- **fruits**, **vegetables**, and everything needed to **cook**.

Each team was **wearing** **chef** **hats** and **aprons**. They **started** **cutting**, **mixing**, **boiling**, and **frying**. The **smells** were **wonderful**! Soon everyone felt **hungry**. We **tasted** the **soup**, **noodles**, and **rice** that others **served**.

At **noon**, the **bell** **rang**. The **hallway** became **crowded** with food **booths** **offering** **samples**. **Judges** were busy **scoring** the **cakes**, **pies**, **bread**, and **cheese**. The **sauce** was **spicy**, **sweet**, and **sour** all at once.

In the **afternoon**, the judges **announced** the **winners**. They **received** **prizes** and **certificates**. All **participants** were **clapping** and **cheering** **loudly**. Everyone **celebrated** the **teamwork** and the **importance** of **cooperation**. **Smiling** **faces** made this a **memorable** day --- **truly** a wonderful festival!

**中文译文**

上周四，可爱的学生们举行了一次烹饪比赛。那个早晨大家都很开心。教室变成了一个忙碌的厨房。有人带来了新鲜的食材------水果、蔬菜，以及烹饪所需的一切。

每个队伍都戴着厨师帽和围裙。他们开始切菜、搅拌、煮汤和煎炒。气味太棒了！很快大家都觉得饿了。我们品尝了别人端上的汤、面条和米饭。

中午，铃声响了。走廊里挤满了提供试吃品的美食摊位。评委们忙着给蛋糕、馅饼、面包和奶酪打分。酱汁集辣、甜、酸于一体。

下午，评委宣布了获胜者。他们收到了奖品和证书。所有参与者都在鼓掌和欢呼。大家庆祝着团队合作和协作的重要性。微笑的面孔让这一天令人难忘------真是一场精彩的美食节！
