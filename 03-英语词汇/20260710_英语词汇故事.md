**The Dance of Courage \| 勇气的舞步**

Two weeks **ago**, my best friend Lily said, \"The school talent **show** is coming! You should join. You can **dance** so well.\" I was **quite** nervous, but I decided not to **refuse**. I wanted to **choose** this opportunity to **express** myself. The performance would **require** serious **train**ing.

From that day on, I woke up **early** every morning to **exercise**. I would **move** my body to the music. My teacher, Ms. Wang, came to **teach** me the steps **carefully**. \"Don\'t **worry** about mistakes. Nobody learns **easily** on the first try,\" she said.

Some days I could **hardly** keep up with the rhythm. A week later, I was **still** stumbling. I wanted to **leave** the practice room and give up. But Ms. Wang said, \"It doesn\'t **matter** if you fall. What matters is that you stand up and **continue**.\" Her words touched my heart.

I went to my friend Tom. He had won a dance contest **recently**. \"Can you **explain** the hardest part **simply**?\" I asked. Tom smiled. \"I won\'t keep you **wait**ing. You just need to **turn** your body at the right moment.\" I watched him and began to **understand** the movement much better.

That afternoon, Mom took me to the mall to **shop** for a costume. We found a lovely dress and bought it quite **cheaply**. \"You look beautiful. I **believe** in you,\" Mom said. We shared a cool **drink** before heading **home**. Her support made me feel strong.

At dinner, I told Dad I wanted to **stay** focused. \"I can **lend** you my camera to record yourself,\" he offered. \"Then you can **review** your moves.\" He also asked if he could **borrow** my notes to learn about the dance. \"**Enjoy** the journey,\" he added. \"You are growing **quickly**.\"

Late that night, I sat by the window and began to **wonder**: what if I fail? I realized I had to **trust** myself. Fear would not stop me from stepping **forward**. I closed my eyes and made a **wish**: please let my dream come true.

The next morning, I felt different. I had **already** decided to give my best. \"This is not **just** a performance,\" I told myself. \"It is a chance to **discover** my true ability.\" My little sister came to **feed** our cat, but she stayed to watch. She even began to **study** my movements with great interest.

In the afternoon, I went to the pool. I love to **swim** when I need a **rest** from practice. As I floated, I thought: I **dare** to dream big. I had learned to **manage** the hardest spin. It was a **surprise** even to myself --- I was finally ready.

The day before the show, I walked **along** the riverside path. I watched the water **cross** beneath the old stone bridge. **Perhaps** everything would be okay. I could not wait for **tomorrow**. Mom called: dinner was ready. She had baked cookies to **encourage** me.

Showtime. Backstage, my heart was racing. I could **either** run or face the crowd. Then I saw Lily in the audience, waving. The music started, and I felt a rush of **excite**ment. I stepped onto the stage, ready to shine.

The spotlight was warm on my skin. I let the rhythm **provide** me with energy. Every leap and spin felt natural. When the music stopped, silence --- then cheers. I could barely **speak**. Lily ran to hug me: \"You were amazing!\"

After the show, my parents embraced me. \"We **compare** this feeling to winning a prize,\" Dad said. Mom added, \"You have **improve**d beyond our expectations. I **admire** your courage.\" Their pride filled my heart. I knew I had **complete**d something meaningful.

That night, I sat in my room to **search** my feelings. I had learned to **protect** my dream with hard work --- to **carry** hope even on the hardest days. My dream had begun to **appear** real. Just weeks before, I had not been able to do this. Now I felt I could **master** anything with enough effort.

**中文译文**

两周前，我最好的朋友莉莉说：\"学校的才艺表演要来了！你应该参加。你跳舞跳得那么好。\"我相当紧张，但我决定不拒绝。我想选择这个机会来表达自己。这次表演需要认真的训练。

从那天起，我每天早早起床锻炼。我会随着音乐活动身体。我的老师王老师来仔细地教我舞步。\"不要担心犯错。没有人第一次尝试就能轻易学会，\"她说。

有些日子我几乎跟不上节奏。一周后我仍然在跌跌撞撞。我想离开练习室，放弃算了。但王老师说：\"如果你跌倒了，那不要紧。重要的是你要站起来继续前进。\"她的话触动了我的心。

我去找我的朋友汤姆。他最近赢了一场舞蹈比赛。\"你能简单地给我解释一下最难的部分吗？\"我问。汤姆笑了笑。\"我不会让你久等。你只需要在合适的时刻转动身体。\"我看着他，开始更好地理解这个动作了。

那天下午，妈妈带我去商场买服装。我们找到了一条可爱的裙子，买得很便宜。\"你看起来很美。我相信你，\"妈妈说。我们在回家前分享了一杯冷饮。她的支持让我感到坚强。

晚饭时，我告诉爸爸我想保持专注。\"我可以借给你我的相机来录自己，\"他主动说，\"然后你可以复习你的动作。\"他还问能不能借我的笔记来了解这种舞蹈。\"享受这个过程，\"他补充道，\"你进步得很快。\"

那天深夜，我坐在窗边开始琢磨：如果我失败了怎么办？我意识到我必须信任自己。恐惧不会阻止我向前迈步。我闭上眼睛许了一个愿：请让我的梦想成真。

第二天早上，我感觉不同了。我已经决定要尽自己最大的努力。\"这不仅仅是一场表演，\"我告诉自己，\"这是一个发现自己真正能力的机会。\"我的小妹妹来喂我们的猫，但她留下来观看。她甚至开始研究我的动作，非常感兴趣。

下午，我去了游泳池。当我需要从练习中休息时，我喜欢游泳。当我在水中漂浮时，我想：我敢于做大梦。我已经学会设法完成最难的那个旋转。这对我自己来说也是一个惊喜------我终于准备好了。

表演前一天，我沿着河边小径散步。我看着水从古老的石桥下穿过。或许一切都会顺利。我迫不及待地等待明天。妈妈打电话来：晚饭准备好了。她烤了饼干来鼓励我。

表演时刻到了。在后台，我的心跳加速。我要么逃跑，要么面对观众。然后我看到了人群中的莉莉，她在挥手。音乐响起，我感到一阵兴奋。我走上舞台，准备闪耀。

聚光灯温暖地照在我的皮肤上。我让节奏给我提供能量。每一次跳跃和旋转都感觉自然。当音乐停止时，先是寂静------然后是欢呼声。我几乎说不出话来。莉莉跑过来拥抱我：\"你太棒了！\"

表演结束后，爸爸妈妈拥抱了我。\"我们把这种感觉比作赢得奖品，\"爸爸说。妈妈补充道：\"你的进步超出了我们的预期。我欣赏你的勇气。\"他们的骄傲充满了我的心。我知道我完成了一些有意义的事情。

那天晚上，我坐在房间里搜寻自己的感受。我学会了用努力保护自己的梦想------即使在最艰难的日子里也要携带希望。我的梦想已经开始变得真实。就在几周前，我还做不到这一点。现在我觉得只要足够努力，我就能掌握任何事情。
