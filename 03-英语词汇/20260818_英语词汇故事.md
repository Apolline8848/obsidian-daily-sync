**一、英文故事**

**Two Days in the Countryside**

Every **weekend**, I get up **early**. This Saturday, I **decide** to be a **volunteer** and go to a small **village** far away. I **choose** to **ride** my bike to the **subway** **station** first.

After I **leave**, I take a **train** for two hours. Then I **cross** a wide **river** over an old wooden **bridge**. Two villagers **offer** to **drive** me the last **kilometer** there.

The **weather** is **sunny** and not **windy**, so the trip is easy. The **pretty** green fields make me happy. Only a few **hundred** people live here. I **spend** a quiet **minute** taking it all in and **enjoy** the fresh air.

The children are shy at first, but soon we **laugh** and **dance** **together**. I **show** them how to **swim** and how to play **chess**. In the evening, I **write** a short **story** and read it to them.

One boy named Li Ming looks **lonely** and never joins us. I **feel** a little **nervous**, because I do not want to make him sad. His mother tells me a **secret**: he is **worried** because he cannot understand the lessons.

I stay **calm** and **patient**. I **prepare** some easy lessons and **encourage** him to try. Li Ming is **brave** and **active** in class now, and he begins to **discover** that he loves English. Day by day, his **knowledge** grows.

On Sunday, Li Ming takes his **exam**. The **result** is **wonderful** --- he passes! I **praise** him and **shake** his hand. His **honesty** and **kindness** move me deeply, and I am very **proud** of him.

Before I go home, the children ask me to **climb** the **mountain** with them. We walk **across** the green hills and see the beautiful view. I **hope** Li Ming will become **famous** one day and make a **difference** in his hometown. He is a **polite** and **honest** boy, and I **admire** his dream.

This short **journey** teaches me the **true** **value** of helping others. I **achieve** something more important than any prize: I learn that **careful** help and a little **attention** can **create** something bright in a child\'s heart.

**二、中文译文**

每个周末，我都起得很早。这个星期六，我决定做一名志愿者，去一个遥远的小村庄。我选择先骑自行车到地铁站。

离开地铁站后，我坐了两个小时的火车。然后我走过一座古老的木桥，穿过一条宽阔的河。两位村民主动开车送我最后一公里。

天气晴朗，没有风，所以旅途很轻松。漂亮的绿色田野让我心情愉快。这里只住着几百人。我花了一小会儿静静欣赏这一切，享受着清新的空气。

孩子们起初很害羞，但很快我们就一起欢笑、一起跳舞。我教他们游泳、下国际象棋。晚上，我写了一个小故事，读给他们听。

一个叫李明的男孩看起来很孤独，从不加入我们。我感到有点紧张，因为我不想让他难过。他的妈妈告诉我一个秘密：他很担心，因为他听不懂功课。

我保持平静，也很有耐心。我准备了一些简单的功课，鼓励他试一试。李明现在在课堂上变得勇敢又积极，他开始发现自己喜欢英语。一天天过去，他的知识不断增长。

星期天，李明参加了考试。结果非常精彩------他通过了！我表扬了他，握了握他的手。他的诚实和善良深深地打动了我，我为他感到非常自豪。

在我回家之前，孩子们请我和他们一起去爬山。我们走过绿色的山丘，欣赏美丽的风景。我希望李明有一天会出名，为他的家乡做出贡献。他是一个有礼貌又诚实的孩子，我钦佩他的梦想。

这段短短的旅程让我懂得了帮助他人的真正价值。我收获的比任何奖励都重要：我懂得了，细心的帮助和一点点关注，就能在一个孩子心中创造出光明。
