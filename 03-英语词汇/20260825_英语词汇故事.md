**一、英文故事**

**A Home for Lucky**

Last **weekend** the **weather** was **cold** and **rainy**. Mei woke up **early** on Saturday, but the **windy** storm kept her in bed until **late** in the morning.

Around noon she heard a soft cry near the old **bridge** at the edge of her **village**. She put on a coat, **crossed** the road, and beside the **river** she found a tiny, **lonely** dog.

The little dog was shaking. Mei felt a little **nervous**, but she was also **worried** about its safety, so she **decided** to take it home. She wrapped it in a **warm** towel and **rode** her bike back in a **minute**.

At home, she **washed** the dog in the **kitchen** and **cooked** a small meal for it. The dog ate the **delicious** soup and looked **pretty** again once its fur was clean.

Mei did not **wonder** long about what to do next. She **prepared** a soft bed and **encouraged** the scared dog to rest. She stayed **calm** and **patient**, and slowly the dog became **brave** and **active**.

She **hoped** to find the dog\'s owner, so she **offered** to make posters. She **drew** a picture of the dog and **wrote** a short note about it, then **shared** the posters with her neighbors.

Her grandma **remembered** a farmer who lived far away, so Mei planned to **leave** the next morning. She took a **train** from the **station** and then **climbed** a small **mountain** to reach the farm.

The farmer was **polite** and **honest**. He told her that Lucky had run away during the storm. He **praised** Mei for her **kindness** and **shook** her hand with a smile.

Before Mei went home, the farmer **taught** her how to look after a young dog. She **listened** carefully and promised to **learn** more, and soon her **knowledge** about animals grew. She also wanted to **volunteer** at the animal shelter one day.

Back home, Mei **spent** the whole afternoon playing with Lucky. The dog could now **wear** a small red collar and run freely in the garden. Mei felt **proud** of what she had done.

She understood the **value** of small acts of care. Helping Lucky showed her that a little **attention** and love can **connect** two hearts and **create** a true friendship.

Mei **discovered** a new **talent** for caring for animals, and she kept a little **secret**: she would **develop** a small shelter for lost pets and **achieve** her dream of helping them all.

She **imagined** a day when every lost animal found a home. This wish gave her a peaceful **mind** and good **health**, because helping others made her heart strong.

On the **journey** home from the farm, Mei and Lucky **laughed** and played **together**. She knew the little dog would never be **bored** again.

**二、中文译文**

上个周末天气又冷又下雨。梅梅星期六起得很早，但狂风暴雨让她一直赖床到上午很晚。

快到中午时，她听到村子边缘那座旧桥附近传来微弱的叫声。她穿上外套，穿过马路，在河边发现了一只小小的、孤独的狗。

小狗在发抖。梅梅有点紧张，但也很担心它的安全，于是她决定把它带回家。她用一条温暖的毛巾把它包起来，一分钟就骑自行车回到了家。

回到家，她在厨房里给狗洗了澡，还为它做了一顿小饭。狗吃完了美味的汤，毛一干净，又变得漂亮起来。

梅梅没有犹豫太久该怎么做。她铺了一张柔软的床，鼓励这只受惊的狗休息。她保持平静和耐心，慢慢地，狗变得勇敢又活泼。

她希望能找到狗的主人，于是主动提出做海报。她画了一张狗的画像，写了一段简短的说明，然后把海报分享给邻居们。

奶奶想起了一位住得很远的农夫，于是梅梅打算第二天一早出发。她从车站坐火车，然后爬上一座小山，来到了农场。

农夫很有礼貌，也很诚实。他告诉梅梅，这只叫 Lucky 的小狗在暴风雨中跑丢了。他称赞了梅梅的善良，并微笑着和她握了握手。

回家前，农夫教梅梅如何照顾小狗。她认真听着，并答应要学习更多，很快她关于动物的知识就增长了。她也想在有一天去动物收容所做志愿者。

回到家，梅梅整个下午都在和 Lucky 一起玩。狗现在能戴上一个小小的红色项圈，在花园里自由奔跑。梅梅为自己所做的一切感到自豪。

她明白了小小善举的价值。帮助 Lucky 让她懂得，一点点关注和爱就能连接两颗心，创造出真正的友谊。

梅梅发现自己有照顾动物的天赋，她还藏了一个小秘密：她要为走失的宠物建一个小收容所，实现帮助所有小动物的梦想。

她想象着有一天，每一只走失的动物都能找到家。这个愿望让她心态平和、身体健康，因为帮助别人让她的心变得强大。

从农场回家的路上，梅梅和 Lucky 一起欢笑、一起玩耍。她知道这只小狗再也不会无聊了。
