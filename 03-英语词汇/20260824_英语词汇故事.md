**一、英文故事**

**A Summer to Remember**

Last **weekend**, Lily went to a music **camp** in the **country**. Her father **drove** **north** **along** a quiet **road** through a green **forest**. She sat **between** her parents and looked out of the window. At a sharp **corner**, they saw a wooden **sign** that read \"Welcome to Green Lake Music Center\".

The center had a **large** **building** and a small **tower**. Behind them there was a **park**, a sandy **beach**, and a **deep** lake. Near the water, Lily found a **cute** little **animal** --- a baby rabbit. The center\'s teacher was a friendly **musician** named Mr. Chen. He could play the **piano**, the **violin**, and the **drum**.

\"Everyone has a special **talent**,\" Mr. Chen said. \"Tonight we will have a **show**. Please **try** your best.\" Lily felt **strange** and shy. She had passed her school **exam** last month, but this felt even harder. She was not **born** with a strong **ability** to sing, but she had a quick **brain** and a **creative** heart. Her **partner** Tom said, \"Let\'s **prepare** a song and dance **activity** together. It will be **fantastic**!\"

They **practiced** every afternoon. At first they made a **terrible** noise, and some children would **laugh**. But Lily stayed **calm** and never gave up. Tom said, \"You are **brave**. That is the **true** **spirit** of a real performer.\"

One night it was almost **midnight**, but Lily could not **lie** still in bed. She was **crazy** with worry. \"What if I **fail**?\" she thought. Then she remembered Mr. Chen\'s words: \"The **value** of music is not only to **win** a prize, but to bring joy to others.\"

The next morning, a boy named Jack lost his **money** and looked very sad. Lily went to **talk** with him kindly. \"Don\'t worry,\" she said. \"Let me help you look for it.\" They searched near the **hospital** and asked people for help, but they could not find it. Lily wanted to **treat** Jack to an ice cream, but she had little **free** time before the performance. Jack smiled and said, \"You are **honest** and **polite**, Lily. You gave me your full **attention** when I needed a friend. That means more than all the coins in the world.\" He **shook** Lily\'s hand warmly. Lily felt a quiet **pride** in her heart.

At last, the evening of the performance came. Everyone **waited** in the big hall. A **message** on the wall read \"Welcome to the Summer Music Night!\" Lily and Tom played and danced. The joyful sound filled the room from **east** to **west**, and the melody floated away to the **south**.

When they finished, Mr. Chen **praised** them. \"You did a wonderful job,\" he said. \"You shared your music and your kindness. That is what makes a **successful** evening.\" Lily and Tom smiled. They did not take the first prize, but they had learned something more precious.

On the last day, they visited a small **museum** and saw many old instruments. They **spent** the whole **holiday** making music and new friends. Lily wrote in her diary: \"This summer taught me to **turn** fear into courage, and to share what I love with others.\"

**二、中文译文**

上个周末，莉莉去乡下的一个音乐营地。她爸爸开车向北沿着一条安静的路，穿过一片绿色的森林。她坐在父母中间，望着窗外。在一个急转弯处，他们看到一块木牌，上面写着「欢迎来到绿湖音乐中心」。

中心有一栋大楼和一座小塔。它们后面有一个公园、一片沙滩和一个很深的湖。在水边，莉莉发现了一只可爱的小动物------一只兔宝宝。中心的老师是一位友好的音乐家，名叫陈先生。他会弹钢琴、拉小提琴，还会打鼓。

「每个人都有特殊的天赋，」陈先生说，「今晚我们要举办一场表演。请尽你们最大的努力。」莉莉感到又奇怪又害羞。她上个月刚通过了学校的考试，但这感觉更难。她天生没有很强的唱歌能力，但她头脑灵活，还有一颗富有创造力的心。她的搭档汤姆说：「我们一起准备一个唱歌跳舞的活动吧。那一定会非常棒！」

他们每天下午都练习。起初他们发出可怕的声音，一些孩子会笑。但莉莉保持冷静，从不放弃。汤姆说：「你很勇敢。那是一个真正表演者的精神。」

一天晚上快午夜了，莉莉还是无法静静地躺在床上。她担心得要命。「万一我失败了呢？」她想。接着她想起陈先生的话：「音乐的价值不只是赢得奖品，而是给别人带来快乐。」

第二天早上，一个叫杰克的男孩丢了钱，看起来非常难过。莉莉好心地过去和他说话。「别担心，」她说，「我帮你找找看。」他们在医院附近寻找，也请人帮忙，但还是找不到。莉莉想请杰克吃冰淇淋，但她在表演前没多少空闲时间。杰克笑着说：「莉莉，你诚实又有礼貌。在我需要朋友的时候，你给了我全部的关注。这比世界上所有的硬币都珍贵。」他热情地和莉莉握了握手。莉莉心中涌起一种平静的自豪。

终于，表演的那个晚上到了。大家都在大厅里等待。墙上有一条消息写着「欢迎来到夏日音乐之夜！」莉莉和汤姆又演奏又跳舞。欢快的声音从东到西充满了整个房间，旋律向南飘去。

他们表演结束后，陈先生表扬了他们。「你们做得很好，」他说，「你们分享了音乐和善良。这才是让一个夜晚成功的原因。」莉莉和汤姆笑了。他们没有拿到第一名，但他们学到了更珍贵的东西。

最后一天，他们参观了一座小博物馆，看到了许多古老的乐器。他们整个假期都在做音乐、交新朋友。莉莉在日记里写道：「这个夏天教会我把恐惧变成勇气，也教会我和别人分享我所爱的东西。」
